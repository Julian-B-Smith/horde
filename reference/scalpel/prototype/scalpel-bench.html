Things to try: hold a chord at coupling 0, then push K toward 1 and hear the members lock into one blade; pull it to −1 and the blades splay, repeating the cut several times per cycle. For the narrow trance saw, load Trance jitter and bring Depth down to blend the jittered saw against the clean one. Either pad axis can drive any continuous parameter. Anti-aliasing is PolyBLEP on every blade edge, sync reset, and carrier wrap (including under FM), plus the optional 2× stage.
