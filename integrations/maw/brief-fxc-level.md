# Brief: what level does horde feed into FX-C?

**The ask.** Shriek's presets are about to be rebuilt so that their default sound is
musical on horde's mix bus, with the Drive macro reaching the heavy territory. Please
tell us the **nominal signal level at FX-C's input**:

1. The typical **loudness or RMS** of a horde patch at that point (for example "−18 dBFS
   RMS", or "about −20 LUFS-S"), and the **peak headroom** you stage for.
2. **Where FX-C sits relative to gain staging**: before or after horde's master fader, and
   after any per-voice or bus gain the mix page (B435) applies.
3. **Whether anything limits or clips before FX-C**, which would bound its peaks.

**For context.** Our current audition program, a drum, sub, bass and chords loop at
−14 LUFS, measures RMS −11 dBFS with transient peaks of +2.2 dBFS. That is as hot as a
mastered track, and probably hotter than your bus. If horde has no fixed figure yet, a
target or a range is enough.

**What we do with the answer.** We re-level the audition material to your figure,
design each kept preset's defaults there, and set the Drive macro's range so a single
voice can still be pushed into the old character. Nothing in the module ABI changes.
