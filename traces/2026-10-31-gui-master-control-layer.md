- **Design choice made here:** ADR-335 r1 keeps "map keys always" as ONE constant in the shell that the human may flip. Putting "always" into the Session unconditionally would have taken that switch away (and made M toggle twice in the shell, and toggle an invisible MAP in two labs). So the Session takes it as an option, off by default; the shell's constant still decides.
