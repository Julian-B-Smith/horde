# b351-alias-refit — the aliasing gate re-fitted on B346's os-convergence estimator and moved to it

- **Queue item:** B351, read verbatim from `origin/lead-records-141:ROADMAP.md` (records PR #849) beside B345, B346 and B350, with ADR-189. The lead decided on the human's delegation ("You can decide whether we re-fit the aliasing cut"): re-fit now, on the heard engine, with broad#383 left out, and move THRESH's aliasing gate to the better estimator measure, provided the vibrato and glide detector controls pass at the new cut.
- **Why:** B350's ruled aliasDb cut (−26.8) failed a must-read-high control. A naive saw at E5 with vibrato reads −27.2 dB on B345's metric, because that metric under-reads moving partials. B346's estimator reads that saw at −22.4 dB, so the cut had to be fitted on the estimator's numbers before it could be a gate.
- **Depends on:** PR #847 (B346, `patchspace-alias-sources`) and PR #846 (B350, `patchspace-thresh-v2`). Both were open when this branch was cut from `origin/main` (d443eb6), and both are merged into it. They merged cleanly.

## The engine

- **Heard engine:** ADR-189's flags (B355) are not on main and no B355 PR exists, so `docs/design/scalpel-horde-engine.js` is the engine the human heard.
- **Render check:** the re-measure asserts the estimator's renders at N and 4N give the page's own aliasDb on every segment, or it throws. It did not throw, so they are the same sound.

## The re-fit

Run: `calibrate.mjs --remeasure --conv` on the human's v1 and v2 exports, read only from the main checkout's `local/patchspace/ratings/`. It took 58 min: 34 patches, 7 segments, 5 renders per segment. broad#383 is left out as CHAOTIC, with B346's 1-ULP evidence, so n = 33 and 12 are rated aliased.

**v2, the worst heard segment (the basis B350 ruled on):**

| measure | AUC | cut (dB) | agreement at cut | leave-one-out | logistic 50% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| aliasDb (B345) | 0.92 | −26.8 | 0.88 | 0.79 | −24.0 |
| **aliasConvDb** | **0.88** | **−33.4** | **0.79** | **0.70** | **−17.8** |
| aliasTotalDb | 0.88 | −25.5 | 0.79 | 0.67 | −15.6 |

**Stability of the cut:**

| measure | cut, heard | cut, committed basis (E5 0.3 s) | cut without the 8 drifted ratings | cut with broad#383 kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| aliasDb | −26.8 | −39.8 (AUC 0.96) | −35.5 | −26.8 |
| **aliasConvDb** | **−33.4** | **−34.9** (AUC 0.94) | **−33.4** | **−33.4** |
| aliasTotalDb | −25.5 | −32.2 (AUC 0.94) | −30.8 | −25.5 |

**v1** (committed numbers, n = 33, 10 aliased): AUC aliasDb 0.75, aliasConvDb 0.84, aliasTotalDb 0.83.

**The choice: aliasConvDb at −33.4 dB.** The in-sample fit favours aliasDb on v2 by AUC +0.04, agreement +0.09 and LOO +0.09, which is 1 to 3 ratings out of 33. On v1 the fit favours the estimator by AUC +0.09. The fit does not clearly say otherwise, so the physically correct estimator wins on these grounds:

1. Its cut moves 1.5 dB between the heard basis and the gauntlet's own window, where aliasDb's moves 13 dB and aliasTotalDb's 6.7 dB. It also does not move when the drifted or chaotic ratings are dropped. A gate applied to the gauntlet's single E5 window needs a cut that does not depend on the window.
2. aliasDb's fitted cut fails the E5 vibrato control (the B350 red). aliasTotalDb's (−25.5) sits above the estimator's reading of the naive A3 vibrato saw (−26.1), so it would fail that must-read-high control too.
3. It is the only one of the three whose fitted cut passes every control.

The logistic midpoint (−17.8) is far above the ROC cut, so the ears' trend is shallow. The cut is where the ears start to disagree with "clean", not where most say "aliased".

## What changed

- **`tools/patchspace/gauntlet.mjs`:**
  - `THRESH.aliasDb` −26.8 is gone. `THRESH.aliasConvDb` is −33.4, with the fit written into the header.
  - `failures()` reads `r.aliasConvDb`, and it throws on a run measured without the estimator rather than pass it silently.
  - `convLeg(patch, seed)` is `measure()`'s B346 leg on its own. `measure()` calls it with the same key order, so runs are unchanged. The report's determinism check reproduced 6 of 6.
  - roughness 0.12, rootPresence 0.616 and noiseDb −21.5 are kept from #846.
  - The aliasing gate is in `failures()`, not `incoherence()`. The brief named `incoherence()`, but that function holds no aliasing gate.
- **`tools/patchspace/calibrate.mjs`:**
  - New `--remeasure --conv` mode, with `convSeg` inside the CLI block: the page's render route, the capture engine, `estimate()` per window, and the worst window. The truth is decimated once, for the last window.
  - The committed basis is augmented in memory through `convLeg`, so the sample file is not written.
  - New `PAIRS_B351` and `CHAOTIC`.
  - `analyse(…, {exclude})` names what it leaves out. The default output is unchanged, and the T8 pins pass.
- **`docs/design/listening-pass.html`** (PURE block):
  - `detectorControls(M, T)`: when `T.aliasConvDb` exists (the live THRESH, T10), the saws' aliased and clean verdicts use the gate's estimator. `convTone` generates the tone at 1–16× the rate and decimates it through one windowed-sinc low-pass, which is metrics_check X's construction.
  - New rows: the A3 vibrato and the E5 and A3 glides, each naive and band-limited.
  - B345's must-read-zero (≤ −90) is still asserted on every clean row.
  - With frozen `SAMPLE.thresholds` (the page's own self-check and its reveal) nothing changes.
  - `detectorLine` prints `conv`. The header note is updated.
- **`tools/patchspace/listening_sample.mjs`:**
  - `strataFor(T)`, `controlsFor(T)`, `gatesOf(T)`, `aliasGateOf(T)` and `stratumLabel`/`strataLabels` replace the constants that read `THRESH.flatness` and `THRESH.aliasDb`.
  - At the committed sample's own thresholds they give exactly the B316 bands and rules.
  - A future draw bands aliasConvDb and noiseDb around the ruled gates. Checked in scratch against the P3 pool: every stratum has 136 or more candidates, and the controls have 21 clean and 159 broken.
  - `remeasure()` now labels roughOrigin with the sample's own roughness threshold, not the live one. Under B350's 0.12 a future `--remeasure` would otherwise relabel items and redden T4.
  - `loadPage` exports `winStarts`.
- **`tools/patchspace/listening_pass_check.mjs`:**
  - New T1 row: every committed stratum label is produced by `strataFor(sample.thresholds)` (30 of 30).
  - CONTROL: the live THRESH's bands do not reproduce them (19 of 30 are not produced).
  - T10's must list adds the six new rows.
  - The check now has 60 checks and 20 controls (it had 58 and 19).
- **`tools/patchspace/gauntlet_report.mjs`, `tools/patchspace/README.md`:** the alias gate is named as aliasConvDb and the comparison section uses its cut. The report was generated from the P3 run data into `local/` (not committed) to exercise it.

## Alternatives rejected

- **aliasDb at its fitted −26.8:** it fails the E5 vibrato control and its cut depends on the basis.
- **aliasTotalDb at its fitted −25.5:** it fails the A3 vibrato control (−26.1), and it has the lowest LOO (0.67).
- **Keeping the frozen B316 aliasing verdicts in T10 alongside the estimator's:** T.aliasDb no longer exists in THRESH. The aliased verdict is now the gate's measure, B345's must-read-zero is kept, and the rows are more numerous.
- **Sharding the re-measure over workers:** not built. One process took 58 min, and a sharded run would have needed new code.
