# armor-soak-first: a first long-run soak, measurement only

- **Queue item:** B448 (Blind-Spot Armor, ADR-197), risk row 5, long-run drift. The overnight brief was written by the lead session on 2026-10-10 after the human's "Go" on the B448 batch.
- **Why:** Row 5 asks for a 4-hour offline render per engine at extreme settings with level, DC and spectral-centroid bounds. Nothing had ever run longer than 600 s. The first step is to find out what the existing tools already say at 4 hours and what they cannot say.
- **What changed:** two files, no code. `docs/armor/soak-2026-10-10.md` (the lane report) and this trace. No source, tool, `verify`, catalogue or dashboard file was touched.
- **Evidence consulted:** `tools/stability_check.cpp` (read in full), `tools/measure_h2_engine.cpp` (`runCell`, `hold`), the headers of `denormal_check`, `sr_check`, `blocksize_check`, `robustness_matrix`, `trajectory_check`, `cpu_bench`, `tools/h2_scenarios.mjs` (longest `blocks(N)` literal), `src/swarm_core.h` (`kMaxV`, `f0cur`), `docs/strategy/blind-spot-armor.md` row 5.
- **Runs (built from `4a3dbaf5e45407590f18ed7cebd2c5ae1e81bc05`, Release):** `stability_check` at 60, 600, 3600 and 14400 simulated seconds, all GREEN: worst RMS drift 0.22 / 0.14 / 0.14 / 0.25 dB (bar 1.0), peak 0.8217 (bar 1.0), 0 non-finite, f0 drift 0.000 ct, max abs Ksm 31.7 (bar 200). The 4-hour run took 1836 s of wall time and 10.2 GB peak footprint. `measure_h2_engine --hold defaults 8` ran 3600 s (609 s wall) and 14400 s (1780 s wall) to completion, but measures wall time only. `denormal_check`, `sr_check`, `blocksize_check` GREEN (no duration flag).
- **Findings:** (1) no time trend in level; window scatter is 0.1 to 0.25 dB. (2) Peak and Ksm running maxima rose slightly between 60 s and 3600 s, then flat. (3) The f0 column reads 0.000 on every patch, including the drift patch, and has no must-fail plant. (4) The tool has one flag (`--seconds`), nine hard-coded patches, no mid-run sample, left channel only, n = 16 not the ceiling of 32. (5) Not measured by any tool: DC, spectral centroid, any engine other than the legacy SAW core, anything about horde 2 over time, note churn. (6) The tool buffers the whole render (about 10 GB at 4 h).
- **Alternatives rejected:** adding flags or a mid-run sampler to `stability_check` (a gate edit and a code change, out of scope tonight); writing a throwaway soak tool for DC and centroid (would invent bounds without a baseline; the report proposes the real tool instead); running `--hold` with a bank preset (preset names were not enumerated overnight).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 at `4a3dbaf` (`.harness/last-verify.json`: `{"target":"fast","exit":0,"git":"4a3dbaf","ts":"2026-10-10T05:59:55Z"}`), the tree being origin/main plus these two docs. The leak check (`git grep` with `.leakcheck-names`) returned nothing. `verify full` was not run (docs only).
- **Open questions:** whether "per engine" means the 1.0 roster or every core; whether the plugin shell is in the soak's scope; widen the frozen tool or build a new one. Listed in the report, section 8.
