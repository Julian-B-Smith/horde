# b585-morph-architecture-blend — the design proposal and its demonstration (B585 steps 1 and 2)

- **Queue item:** B585 (ADR-317), steps 1 and 2. Acceptance, verbatim: "(1) a design proposal (`docs/proposals/morph-architecture-blend.md`) answering, each with options, costs and a recommendation: how many architectures may sound at once at a point inside the pad (two, or up to four); the granularity of the dual render (per structural group such as a blade, a filter, a source or an FX slot, or the whole voice); the blend region (the whole pad's weights, or a band around each key's seeded point); the CPU budget (PF-4) and what happens past it; how Quantum and the stepped hybrid place each structural key's flip; how a parameter's own morph mode (B557) overrides it; voices that start inside a blend region; the hosted FX modules (a module's declared `switch`, ADR-309) across the pad; (2) a lab demonstration on a stand-in voice (the Random lab's stand-in patch may serve) of Blend across a structural difference, heard and measured".
- **Why:** the human ruled that architecture follows the morph law (ADR-317, "Architecture follows the law too"). Today IF-4 flips structure at the seeded point under every law. The change is designed before it is specified.

- **Measured** (the page's own core, run headless in Node by a scratch script that extracts the page's core block, with no copy; the same numbers show on the page):
  - **Default case** (Sync + LP at A, FM + BP at B; A→B in 3 s): the click reading is +8.6 dB for the bare jump, +0.2 for today's flip, +0.0 for Blend. The switch reading is +1.2 dB for today's flip against −46.1 for Blend. Structure renders per voice while moving are 2.00 today and 4.00 under Blend.
  - **Stepped at 4 Hz:** switch −23.2 dB.
  - **Equal-gain level dip:** −3.4 dB at 0.65 of the path (two settings), and −6.7 dB at the centre of the diagonal (four blades).
  - **"At most two":**
    - on the diagonal with four distinct corners, a −15.8 dB switch at the centre (B and C tie, so only A, then only D);
    - a +5.9 dB bump near the centre;
    - PA-1's glide overlaps a third setting for 148 ms of the y = 0.4 sweep (self-check C4).
  - **Self-checks:** C1 to C6 PASS.
  - **Visual check:** a headless Chrome screenshot of the page, default view and `#law=flip&kind=four&path=AD`. Six of six self-checks show on the page, and the pad map paints the seeded edges.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - Linking the proposal from the demo page: `serve_labs_check` refuses a lab link outside the lab trees (404). The path is shown as text.
  - Naive window edges (the Random lab's): a moving hard edge quantised to whole samples swamped the switch reading (−18.7 dB for Blend, from the stand-in's own aliasing). polyBLEP edges bring it to −46.1 dB.
  - A hard cap of two in the demo: the overlap is reported instead, as a finding about what a cap of two costs (it needs a slot rule).
