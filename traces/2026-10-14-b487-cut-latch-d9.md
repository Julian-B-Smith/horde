- **Why:** under a cut rule (or a Cut spread) with Quantize on, the spread law's walk flips each member's slot between whole values; the engine fed the new cut to the blades at the tick, mid-cycle, and a blade's carrier phase is the cut times the member's phase into the window in closed form, so every flip was a jump in the wave (60 % of Glass horde pad's crackle at C3, B485). D9 holds the law's new cut as pending and applies it at that blade's next window entry, where its carrier phase restarts. A flag in the C++ engine and in the composed JS, default OFF, so nothing a listener hears changes and no existing digest moves.

## The cycle-start event, per blade mode

One event for every mode: the step on which the member's phase passes the blade's window start, the blade law's own window entry (`e1 < e0`; razor-core.js stepM :655-662, the `entered` that fmStep reads; engine.h stepBlade). On that step the window-relative phase is `e1 < dphi`, so the carrier phase `hp = kk·e1` (voicePlainK; razor-core.js voice :183) has just restarted from 0.

| mode | what restarts at the window entry | the latch there |
|---|---|---|
| 0 sync | the carrier phase `hp + xin + cacc` | applied at the entry |
| 1 FM reset | the carrier phase, and the modulator (`mEff·er`) and the pitch-FM integrator (`ns.acc = 0`) | applied at the entry |
| 2 FM free | the carrier phase `hp`; the modulator runs free and the pitch-FM integrator is not reset (it integrates `kk·dphi`, so a new cut changes only its later increments, never its value) | applied at the entry |
| 3 noise | the hold index `floor(hp)` restarts, and a new draw is taken | applied at the entry; a cut changed mid-window would move `floor(hp)` and so the draw times (and, through the shared host stream, the spread's walk) |
| 4 fold | the window; fold reads the cut as a GAIN (`1 + (k-1)/4`), not a phase | applied at the entry, where the window's own edge already is (under Hard > 0 the gate is 0 there) |
| 5 ring | the carrier phase | applied at the entry |
| 6 crush | the hold steps `floor(hp)` and their averages | applied at the entry |

**No mode has a carrier that never restarts**, so no other rule was needed. Two stated edges of the one rule: (a) a restart the WINDOW makes, moving back over the phase (rotation, or a position the walk moves), is not an entry by the blade law's test, which reads entry from the phase's own advance; the cut then waits for the next entry (measured: 14.6 ms, two cycles, at worst on Glass horde pad's blade 2 at C3, whose Position spread the walk moves). (b) Where the window moves FORWARD faster than the phase (rotation faster than the member's own frequency: possible only on notes under about 10 Hz), that test reads an entry on every step, the oracle's FM reset with it, and the cut is taken at once, as with the flag off.

## The rules, each PROPOSED (the records give "latch a member's new quantized ratio until its blade next starts a cycle" and no more)

1. **Only under Quantize** (`kq`), the one case in which the law steps the cut (D8's stepped kinds 1 and 2: `kAdd`, `kMul`, and blade 2's `kAdd2`, `kMul2`). Without Quantize nothing changes (the row `C/D9 Quantize off: nothing is latched` renders as the flag off).
2. **Several flips before one cycle start: the latest wins.** Measured: on the probe job at C3 the latched engine changes its fed cut 985 times in 4 s on blade 1 against the law's 3384, so about 3.4 of the law's flips fall inside one cycle and are never heard.
3. **SET at once, as D8 sets:** a note's first `spread()` (a fresh voice), the first after the flag or Quantize turns on, a member that has just joined (N raised), one still waiting on onset scatter (silent), and blade 2 while it is off (nothing reads its cut). **A note-on on a voice already sounding (a mono retrigger) is NOT a cycle start:** its new cut waits like any other, which is D8's own rule for that call. (The brief read "a note-on: set at once, as D8 sets at the note-on's own computation": D8 sets only on a fresh voice and keeps its ramps on a retrigger; this follows D8 in both.)
4. **Bounded latency:** one blade cycle where the window holds still, the member's period: 30.6 ms at C1 (32.7 Hz), 7.6 ms at C3, 1.9 ms at C5. At C1 a cut flip can therefore lag the walk by up to 30.6 ms, and the cut changes at most about 33 times a second; the sound keeps one cut for each whole cycle. With a moving window, up to about two cycles (above).
5. **With D8:** D8 SETS the cut under Quantize (ADR-216 A4 item 2) and the latch then holds it; with D8 off the latch holds the law's own step. They do not interact otherwise (the law's cut is taken before `rampSpread`, so D8's retrigger rule cannot hide it).
6. **The DC estimate** taken on the very sample a latch applies keeps the cut that sample began with: the composed JS's render holds its own copy of blade 1's cut for it, and the C++ matches (renderCallK's `kq`). It refreshes every 256 samples and is smoothed by 0.003, so this is a one-estimate lag.
7. **The bound of the judged share:** 5 % of the flag-off hits at a tick boundary, on at least 100 (D8's, ADR-216 A4 item 12); D8's boundary set (positions 0-1 at os 1, 0-6 at os 2). Measured margin: the engine 0 %, the latch-ignored plant 100 %.
