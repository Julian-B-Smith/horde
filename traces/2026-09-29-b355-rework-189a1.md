- **`docs/design/scalpel-horde-engine.js`:**
  - **D1**: `d1Takes(p, aa)`. D1 takes a blade's carrier only when:
    - the carrier shape is not sine (nor sine→saw), AND
    - the blade is an FM mode (1/2) OR Band-limit is off, AND
    - the modulator is not S&H (mshape 7).
  - The S&H rule binds FM blades only, because a sync or ring blade has no modulator.
  - `voiceAA` and `scan()` share the rule. Outside it the carrier keeps its BLEPs, bit for bit (AA1s).
  - **D3**: the ORACLE'S tap (`y`, the PolyBLEP output) through a one-pole at `LOOP_FC` = **5 kHz**. The tap change is gone.

  - `?aa189=0` turns all three off; `?d1=0`, `?d2=0` and `?d3=0` turn off one each.
