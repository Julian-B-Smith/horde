# b584-gui3-slice6: the MAP control in the top bar, the Set tab, envelope readouts that follow, mixer room

- **Queue item:** B584 slice 6 (ADR-261, ADR-333, B593). Built on slice 5 (`gui3-b584-slice5`, horde #1291, not yet merged); the PR goes against `main` and its diff includes slice 5 until #1291 merges.
- **Acceptance (the lead's brief), in short:**
  1. The bundle's MAP control and live source strip in the top bar, identical on every page. MAP on, a click or drag on a knob makes or edits a mapping per the spec's table, written as a DEVICE route (history, matrix, mirror), not only in the session. M, Esc, 1 to 9, P, Backspace each with a mouse route. Page changes keep MAP and the source. Sources read 0 and are labelled MOCK.
  2. The Set tab opens Settings.
  3. An oscillator view's Envelope readout shows the envelope its source follows.
  4. More padding around the mixer's elements and more room left of the osc toggles; tokens only.
  5. The Ledger stays its own tab; the `quick-map` and `settings` rows updated.
  6. Seam-check tests with must-fail plants for 1 to 3; the generator's controls stay red as they must.
  7. `./verify fast` green; screenshots; this trace; a PR.
- **Why:** the human, 2026-10-30. The quick map selector they meant is the design system's own Map Mode (`docs/design-system/HORDE-Map-Mode-Spec.md`, byte-identical to their downloads), not a lab. The Ledger "can stay its own tab until it isn't needed at all anymore". "Yes, send the next slice" (the Set fix and the envelope readout). And "a little extra padding around the elements" on the mixer, with "a little more space on the left" of the osc toggles.
- **What changed:**
  - **`docs/design/gui3.html`:**
    - A MAP row in the window's top bar: `H.MapControl` (toggle joined to the source dropdown), a MOCK tag and `H.SourceStrip` (one scrolling line of the device's 16 sources). The window's border takes the source's colour while MAP is on (the bundle's `frame`).
    - `H.ModInspector` docked at the right while MAP is on (the spec's mouse routes for P, Backspace and focus) and absent otherwise.
    - The shell builds its session once and attaches the bridge below; the old per-rev mirror of routes into the session is the bridge's `frame()`.
    - The page bar's groups come from `navGroups`; an Envelope section's readout comes from `followedSummary`.
    - The console's scoped override: strips 104 px (from 92), padding from `--space-gap`, `--space-section`, `--space-dense`, extra left padding on the strip's head; the summed views are 64 px tall and the console's note is one line, to give some of the room back.
    - Three self-checks (MAP on the real knob handlers, the Set tab's click, the envelope readout), making 20; a `,map` review flag.
  - **`docs/design/gui3/shell-logic.js` (new, 83 lines):** `navGroups`, `followedSummary` and `attachMap`, held out of the page so the seam check can run them. `attachMap` writes the session's working list (the bundle edits `S.mappings` in place and tells no one) into the device at each gesture's end and reads the device's routes back; never during a drag; a refusal is said and drops the mapping on the read-back.
  - **`docs/design/gui3/device.js`:** `syncRoutes(list)`: one history step for the whole difference, a new item through the same RC-4 refusal as `addRoute` (the refusal is now `routeFault`, shared; the existing plants' anchors still sit on its first line), `routes()` carries a Control's lo and hi.
  - **`tools/labharness/gui3_seam_check.mjs`:** a small fake DOM (no HTML parsing), the bundle loaded on it (read only), and eight tests with eleven plants: `mapclick`, `mapdrag`, `mapkeys`, `mapcontrol`, `mappage`, `maprefused`, `settab`, `envsummary`. 29 tests and 34 plants in all (from 21 and 23). The tests drive the design system's real `Session`, `Knob`, `MapControl`, `SourceStrip` and `PageNav`.
  - **`tools/gen_gui3_controls.py`:** `shell-logic.js` is read by the determinism gate with the other scripts (no clock). Still 27 must-fail controls, all red.
  - **`docs/design/gui3/port-tab.json`:** the human's words about the Ledger in its note; `quick-map` is `partial` with a reason (`ds-component`) and an honest `missing` list; `settings` says the click works. **`shell-data.js`** regenerated (the ledger travels in it); **`README.md`**, **`docs/design/index.html`** (regenerated from the page's tagline).
- **Evidence consulted:** `docs/design-system/HORDE-Map-Mode-Spec.md` and `HORDE-Map-Mode.html` (read only); the bundle's `Session`, `gesture`, `MapControl`, `SourceStrip`, `ModInspector` and `PageNav` (`docs/design/arp-face/bundle.js`, read only); slice 5's trace; `device.js`, `engine-stub.js`, the generator and the seam check as they stood.
- **Measured:**
  - **Browser:** the page's 20 self-checks all OK in headless Chrome (the MAP check clicks the real knob's handlers with a synthetic pointer; the Set check clicks the real tab).
  - **Fit** (the `fit` flag, bank shown): Main and the Dashboard fit (436 px area, it lost 36 px to the MAP row); Mix overflows by 20 px (456 in 436) and fits with the bank hidden (509 in 509). The extra padding and the MAP row cost that room; the Osc pages overflow as before.
  - **Seam check:** 29 tests, 34 plants each red for its own reason (`--verbose`).
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Writing the mapping hook into the bundle** (an `onMap` event): the bundle is vendored and read only. The need is listed as proposal 22 and in the `quick-map` row.
  - **Mounting the mappings in the device only and drawing rings from it**: the bundle's knobs draw from the session's `mappings`; reconciling at the end of each gesture keeps every ring, caption and count the bundle's own.
  - **A node test that parses `gui3.html`'s inline script for the Set tab:** the fault was in a decision the page made, so the decision moved to `shell-logic.js` and the real Page nav runs on the fake DOM; only the page switch's "never touches MAP" is a structural (text) test, because it is the page's own code (the in-page self-check clicks it).
  - **The inspector as an always-on rail:** it would take 214 px from the pages permanently; it docks only while MAP is on.
- **Verify:** `nice -n 10 ./verify fast`, run last on the committed tree; its tail is in the report and the PR body. The git hash is in `.harness/last-verify.json`.
