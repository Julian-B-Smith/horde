# b642-morph-dropouts — why the test plug-in's morph drops out on held notes

- **Queue item:** B642 (branch `b641-morph-dropouts`, named before B641 went to preset tiers, ADR-382; the horde lead, 2026-11-07, from the human's run of test build 9, "engine 6cd98b5, build 9", in Live: "Morph is a little glitchy right now in any case. It is subject to drop-outs on continuous notes. Worth debugging in detail."). A diagnosis, not a fix: the engine is the builder's.
- **What changed** (`tools/engine-preview/`; only the per-overrun log lines reach what the human plays):
  - `morph_probe.cpp` (`preview-morph-probe`): holds a chord through the plug-in's own CLAP interface while the page's `position` messages move the pad over four corners that differ; times every block (wall and thread CPU), reads each frame's changed engine values and morph levels, and reports drop-outs with the blocks around them. `--pair` runs two instances side by side for paired timing and a bit-for-bit comparison.
  - `morph_hole.cpp` (`preview-morph-hole`): the minimal reproduction of the silent class, on the clean room's resolver alone.
  - `preview_plugin.cpp`: one compile-time guard, `HORDE_PREVIEW_PROBE_WHOLE_SPANS`, for the measurement build `preview-morph-wholespan`. The guard changes nothing in the shipped build (build 9's CLAP and this branch's were bit-identical on the Quantum run).
  - **Per-overrun log lines** (the bridge proposal from the findings, made at the lead's request): each overrun is written by the audio thread into a preallocated ring (`OverrunRing`, 1024 records; a full ring is counted as lost) and drained on the main thread at every `write_stats`, then written after the per-preset totals, which keep their format: `at_s`, preset, frames, share of the block, the morph's state (`off`, `still`, `moving`) and both unison counts as the engine holds them. At most 4096 lines per session, the rest counted. The bridge extension gains a test-only `overrun_at`, so smoke can count every sounding block as an overrun; its check holds the lines to the totals, and the plant `preview-plant` (now also `HORDE_PREVIEW_PLANT_NO_OVERRUN_LINES`) must read as missing lines.
  - `CMakeLists.txt`, `README.md`: the three targets, the plant's new fault, and how to run and read them.
  - `docs/armor/tolerances.json`: the probe's and the reproduction's switch-reading comparisons registered (`inline-float-compare` in `morph_probe.cpp` and `morph_hole.cpp`), on the human's ruling relayed by the lead: "Register them". An earlier draft had moved the literals into named constants, which the check names as a blind spot; they are back as plain literals.
- **Findings** (the probe's runs on engine 6cd98b5, Trance jitter, chord held 24 s, blocks 128 and 512 at 44.1 and 48 kHz):
  - **Spec class, the only silence found:** under Quantum (D.2's default law) each source's switch picks its own corner (MO-26 (3), MO-7, cohesion 0.3), so the pad has stretches where every switch picks a corner its source is off in. A held chord goes to exact zero for 350 to 560 ms, four times in 24 s, at every rate and block size, on build 9's own binary too. `preview-morph-hole`: with A = osc 1 only and B = osc 2 only, D.2's defaults leave about 73 % of the A-B edge silent; 4 of 10 seeds have a hole at cohesion 0.3; none at cohesion 1; Blend none.
  - **CPU class, no bug:** with the morph moving under Blend or Stepped the block costs about 1.6 times morph off (Blend keeps every corner's sources sounding inside the pad: 1 source 23 %, 3 sources 38 % mean at 8 notes); a still morph costs +13 %; Structural flips add little (39.5 % against 33.9 % mean). At 8 notes the worst block reached 97 % of a 512-sample block in thread CPU, 118 % on a busier run: overruns in a host. The bridge's span cut is not the cost: whole spans are bit-identical (DT-2) and were 17 % dearer in a paired run. The page's refused `set` flood costs nothing measurable and changes no sample.
  - **No bridge or engine audio bug found:** no gap but the Quantum holes in any law, rate or block; the house click detector finds no event in any render (its control: 50 ms hard gaps planted are caught; 0.05 x peak steps are not, as its header says for a detuned chord).
  - **The human's log** (`session-20261107-011102`, build 9): Trance jitter, 25,516 sounding blocks of 512, mean 19.42 %, worst 431.26 %, 275 overruns. The mean matches the probe's 4-note Blend (17 to 19 %); the worst block does not, and the log has no timeline, so its overruns can't be split between the morph, unison 11 and the host.
- **Evidence consulted:** SPEC-ENGINE MO-7, MO-13, MO-26, PA-5, IF-12; the engine export's `core/morph/resolver.cpp`, `core/api/engine.cpp`, `core/voice/voice.cpp`; the bridge's `render()`; `docs/design/gui3/device.js` (what the page sends on a pad move); the plug-in's session logs.
- **Alternatives rejected:** removing the bridge's span cut (measured: no saving); judging discontinuities by the click detector alone (it is blind to small steps in this material).
- **Checks:** bridge_test GREEN; smoke GREEN with all three plants (the overrun lines: 40 of 40, 20 moving and 20 still; the control reads as missing lines); the window test GREEN; `./verify fast` (see the commit).
- **Rulings since:** the Quantum silence is the spec's, ruled "Switches pick together" (ADR-383, spec round 64 to come). The CPU finding is a PF-4 budget matter.
