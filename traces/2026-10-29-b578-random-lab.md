# b578-random-lab — the Random page's workshop lab and its report

- **Queue item:** B578 (ADR-312). Acceptance: "the lab plays each level on a stand-in patch, seeded and repeatable, and a report lists what a basic 1.0 page could contain, for the human to rule."
- **Why:** the human asked for a lab to try a Random page: randomize architecture and parameters at every level, mod mappings drawn from rulesets saved as presets, bell-curve bounds around home with a home-to-chaos knob, amount sliders, and snapshots on an XY grid like Sluice's and Shriek's. Basic 1.0 is set from the lab, by the human (ADR-312).

- **Measured (headless, the lab's own functions in Node, seeds 1–60; quoted in the report §4):**
  - **Module ARCH at chaos 0.35 changes nothing on 42–95% of clicks.** This led to the "ARCH must change" proposal (C11: 0/40 versus 38/40).
  - **The first chaos law (4·c·200^(c−1)) moved parameters 0.3% of their range at chaos 0.1.** It was replaced by base 50 (0.9% at 0.1, 4.1% at 0.25, 18% at 0.5).
  - **Randomize patch at chaos 1:** 27% engage the −6 dBFS guard, and 7% are near-silent (causes: a high HP cutoff on sub-only patches, a slow amp attack, near-zero levels).
  - **Rulesets:** 0 rule violations in 327 routes. "Wobble and motion" lost 113 tries to source contention. The PWM rule found no live destination on an all-saw, blade-off patch (355 tries, 0 routes).
  - **ARCH on each corner:** 0 structural keys differ between corners with scope "all four"; 37.3 differ with scope "selected corner".
  - **Drift after 10 clicks:** walk 0.107 versus anchored 0.052.
  - **A headless Chrome run** clicked every level's ARCH and PARAMS and the grid, ruleset and lock buttons: no error, and the replay matched (42 actions).
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **An AudioWorklet** instead of a ScriptProcessor: it would need the engine as a string blob, which means two copies of one renderer. One renderer serves the page and the checks (L0064).
  - **Loading `arp-face/bundle.js` for `Horde.XYPad` and `Horde.Knob`:** they need a `Horde.Session` the lab does not model. The pad and knob are drawn with the bundle's CSS classes (`.hd-xy`, `.hd-chip`, the corner glyphs) instead.
  - **A wheel on the knob:** B301; a plain wheel only scrolls, and this lab registers no wheel listener.
  - **Adding a `Random` part to `gen_lab_index.py`'s PARTS:** out of scope (`tools/`). The lab is tagged "Morph and state".
