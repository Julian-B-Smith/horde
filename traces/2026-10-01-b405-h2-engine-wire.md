# b405-h2-engine-wire — the composed engine's parity check wired into `./verify full` under ADR-187 A2

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B405 (ADR-187 A2, recorded in PR #894 on `lead-records-159`). Brief from the horde lead
  session, 2026-10-01.
- **Why:** The human ruled on the Cross-mod ring (watch) rows (A2 item 1), on the floor's form (item 2) and on
  the landing policy (item 3). That ruling was all the check was waiting for, so it now gates.
  - **Item 1.** The three ring rows, pinned by name, are judged on four things:
    - a strict 1e-6 max-abs over a MEASURED onset window (384 frames);
    - identical events;
    - identical readouts;
    - bounded (|x| ≤ 1, the tanh output stage's range) and finite over the whole render.

    Rules 1a and (b), with `--chaotic-rule` and `--chaotic-k`, are retired. An unknown `--` option now exits 2,
    so a retired flag cannot be read as a stream path.
  - **The window.** Measured over the critic's 25 nudges × 3 rows. Candidates were the powers of two from 128 and
    the block-aligned 384.
    - 384 is the longest under ~1e-8 (worst 3.29e-9, chord at nudge 4).
    - 512 fails at 1.09e-4 (nudge 3).
    - The table is in `docs/port/h2-engine.md`, "The onset window".
  - **The new controls.**
    - **X1-late** (fault 14): X1 from frame 128. It must turn ≥ 1 NON-chaotic xm row red, and its first 128
      frames must be bit-identical to clean on every xm row. It fires on 4 of 35 rows, each 1.02–1.45e-6 (the
      critic's four), and 38 of 38 prefixes are identical.
    - **BF**: a NaN, then 2.0, planted in each ring row's tail. It must fire through bounded-and-finite alone.
      It does, on 3 of 3 rows.
    - **FLOORKEY**: the floor's one verdict function, fed fabricated keys every run. An unkeyed platform and
      another Node major print only; keyed, 29.9% is red and 30% holds.
    - X1 is kept, and still turns 3 of 3 ring rows red.
  - **Item 2.** The floor already gated only on its key and printed elsewhere. It is now decided in one function
    shared by main, the FMA control and the self-test.
- **Evidence consulted:**
  - `tools/h2_engine_parity_check.cpp`, `tools/h2_engine_stream.h`, `tools/h2_engine_render.mjs` (the `--nudge` and
    `CHAOTIC`/`SELF` paths), `h2/engine/engine.h` (`evSample`, the `H2E_EPS13` sites);
  - `docs/port/h2-engine.md` (the 25-nudge ratio method), `verify` (the `h2_scalpel_*` wiring idiom),
    `tools/test_table_check.py` (the WIRED/UNWIRED contract);
  - ROADMAP B405 on `origin/lead-records-159`; ADR-187 A2 as quoted in the brief.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **256 frames as the window.** It is the longest power of two, with a wider margin (8.06e-11 flat). The ruling
    says the longest window that is measured under ~1e-8, and the brief admits block-aligned windows; 384 is both.
    256 is recorded as the fallback.
  - **A new frame counter for X1-late.** The engine's `evSample` already counts every rendered sample, the fade
    path's one-sample calls included, so the hook is one line in the macro.
  - **Routing the start frame through `replay()`.** `tools/h2_engine_stream.h` was out of scope, so the frame is a
    constant beside the fault (`Engine::kX1LateFrame`).
  - **An env override to fake the floor key.** A variable that turns a gate off in the shipped check is a
    weakening vector. The in-process self-test exercises the same function every run without one.
- **Finding (an observation, not a gate):** X1 and X1-late converge to the SAME faulted trajectory on the ring rows.
  |late − X1| falls from 2.6e-6 to 4e-8 over the render, while both stay 0.15–0.46 from clean (a scratch probe,
  not committed). A 1e-9 change to xm flips the ring onto a nearby branch. It is not continuous amplification. The
  384 window catches X1-late on chord (between frames 256 and 384), but not on repeat or arp: that is the blind spot,
  printed every run.
- **Verify:** both runs were on the working tree, i.e. base `8a9bf4d` plus this diff, before the commit; so
  `.harness/last-verify.json` reads git `8a9bf4d`.
  - `./verify fast`: exit 0 (`{"target":"fast","exit":0,"git":"8a9bf4d","ts":"2026-10-01T23:35:45Z"}`).
  - `./verify full`: exit 0 (`{"target":"full","exit":0,"git":"8a9bf4d","ts":"2026-10-01T23:46:49Z"}`), 599.9 s wall
    at load average 6–7. The new pair prints `h2_engine_parity_check: GREEN — 540/543 …, 0 red` and
    `h2_engine_fma_control: FIRED — … 24 of 543 … mean bit-exact 17.48% is under the keyed floor`.
  - Standalone at load 2–3: the check takes 42.1 s and the FMA control 39.3 s. `test_table_check` counts 83 WIRED
    and 1 UNWIRED, all verified.
