# b448-p3-fuzz-second-rework — the fuzz after a critic's review: honest controls, a stated reach, and counts of what a case could show

- **Queue item:** B448 (Blind-Spot Armor), phase 2 Wave 1, package P3 (ADR-209, ADR-211). A second rework of draft PR #1059, same branch, ordered by the lead after a critic's review. The proposal is `docs/strategy/blind-spot-armor-phase2.md` section 1 row R6 and section 3 row P3.
- **Corrects** `traces/2026-10-11-b448-p3-fuzz-reads-above-the-rail.md`. Its control list (four plants), its growth rule ("a case above the pass line is probed"), its linearity threshold (`linTol`), its "judged for silence" counts, its key counts and its run-time numbers describe commit `7127331` and no longer hold. Its account of why the divided-gain reading is valid, and the first trace's points 1 and 3 (the sampler is restated; 21 keys have no declared range), still do.
- **Why:** the review showed the gate could pass without running, that its controls proved its rules only on easy faults, and that its counts claimed more than the cases could show. Each finding below was reproduced or accepted, fixed, and given a control that goes red without the fix.
- **What changed, by finding:**
  1. **A run that never happened read as a pass.** The entry test compared `import.meta.url` with a hand-built `file://` string, so from a path holding a space, or through a symlink, the three scripts ran nothing, printed nothing and exited 0. Fixed in `tools/h2_fuzz_check.mjs`, `h2/harness/selftest.mjs` and `h2/harness/gen_patches.mjs`: both sides are now file URLs of the real path. The fuzz also always ends its standard output with one verdict line, `h2_fuzz_check: GREEN (...)` or `h2_fuzz_check: RED (...)`, on every way out, and a run that judged no case is RED.
  3. **The growth rule's reach.** The blow-up plant now starts at a healthy level (0.5 x gain). A case is probed when the stretch that holds its peak rises by more than `riseProbe` from its third quarter to its last, whatever its level, or when its level is above the pass line. The probe holds that stretch longer by 0.5 s or a third of its own length, whichever is more. One case in 16 has a long hold: 4 s in the PR-size run, 12 s in the deep run. Three more plants: one doubling every 400 ms (only a long hold with the longer probe decides it; the PR-size run must find it), one that doubles and then stops at 64 x gain between the pass and fail lines (must read grey, never red: once flat it is a level), and periodic bursts that never grow (must read clean; it is the plant that needs "the smallest of three ratios"). The run prints the slowest doubling its holds can decide and, per growing plant, the shortest time in the corner at which it was read and the longest at which it was not.
  4. **Cases that could show nothing.** `gain` is kept off its minimum except in its own preamble case, its sweeps and the stacked changes it leads. The run counts, from each render, the cases that could show a level or growth (the bus left zero) and silence (the tail was rendered to the end AND there was sound when the keys came up). A case outside gain's own with a bus of exact zeros is red. `control/silent` must read zero in both counts.
  5. **The reading through the divided gain** is now checked in every case it is used in, at no extra render: each direct output sample against `tanh(reading)`, to within `readUlps` units in the last place. A case outside it is a `misread`, red and named. `control/fuzz-keyed` (a bus multiplied by 8 above 10) must be reported in every case that takes it there. The linearity control uses the same comparison; `linTol` is gone. The source guard stays as a second line and the header says it is textual and can be evaded.
  6. **Judge mutations that left the run green.** Every plant's fault is on the right channel only. Two more plants: a NaN only after the release, and a stuck level that stops 0.65 s after the release (silent at the end of the render and not before). Silence was already judged over the whole tail after the settle time; the late plant is what holds the code to that. `planted()`, which predicts each plant's reading, shares no code with the judge.
  7. **Full-length tails** are chosen by the case's place in its round plus the round's number, so over 8 rounds every key's sweep, stacked change and static place has one, whatever the table's size. The deep run is red unless every key had all three.
  8. **Keys counted as covered that could not change the output.** One case in four strikes a second note (over the held ones, or after a gap). A value counts as sent only if it was in force while a frame rendered. An effect test on a fixed sample (up to 64 patches, independent of the seed) moves each key alone between its extremes: a key with no effect is not counted as reached and is red, unless the engine's source never reads it, which is its own printed class. The 21 keys with no declared range stay a printed HOLE.
  12. Leftovers removed: the stale `MIN_SEGMENT` comment (the rule is now 128 frames, with its reason), the header's 19.27, `away`, `c.mode`, four unused measurement floors.
  - `h2/harness/control_fuzz.h` is rewritten: ten plants, their corners in one table. `h2/harness/adapters.h` has their ten rows. `h2/harness/README.md` lists the file and the plants.

- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - Full-length tails by a hash of the case index: by arithmetic (336 places, one chance in eight per case, 59 rounds) some place would be missed at roughly one seed in eight, and the deep run's rule would then be red for no fault. The place-plus-round rule cannot miss.
  - Probing every audible case for growth: an estimated three times the engine's render time (two longer renders per case), over the PR-size budget.
  - One growth ratio instead of three: the burst plant shows any single ratio calls periodic bursts a blow-up; the smallest of three does not.
  - A gain threshold for "audible": a number with no source. What a case could show is read from its own render instead.
  - Counting a key with no effect as merely printed: the run's rule is that every extreme is reached, and an extreme nothing can hear is not reached.
  - Raising the growth lines to x1.5 and x3 of the new worst: a loosening, left to the human.
  - Running the controls over only the first rounds of a deep run, to halve its time: it would relax what the nightly judged before. Left as a question.
