# b584-gui3-slice2 — GUI 3, second slice: morph off, corner snapshots, the morph toggle in history, mirror and confirm, the widget rule

- **Queue item:** B584, second slice (ADR-319 items 1, 2, 3 and 5). ROADMAP B584, verbatim: "the next slice builds morph off (B to D silent and frozen; the audible corner chosen on the morph page, default A), save current sound to a named snapshot and save to [corner], the morph toggle as a history node, mirror and confirm behind a swappable transport, and the widget rule." The lead's brief added tests with plants for each.
- **Why:** the human answered the first slice's questions (ADR-319), and this slice builds those answers into the shell, the adapter and their gates. Builds on the first slice's trace, `traces/2026-10-29-b584-gui3-shell.md`.
- **What changed:**
  - `docs/design/gui3/device.js`: rewritten around a transport.
    - Morph off: only the audible corner sounds and takes writes; a write to a frozen corner is refused. The audible corner is chosen with `setAudible`, A by default.
    - The morph toggle and the audible corner are in the undo state; the position is not.
    - Corner names and saved presets, with `saveSoundTo`, `savePreset`, `edited` and `revert`.
    - Mirror and confirm: `shown` reads the adapter's own values. A snapshot changes the display only for engine-originated values, never for a stale snapshot (older than the key's latest send) and never for the key being dragged.
    - Scatter is refused while morph is off.
  - `docs/design/gui3/engine-stub.js`: `GUI3Engine.TRANSPORT` (`send`, `onSnapshot`) and `SyncTransport`, which carries batched set messages, gesture messages and snapshots with `ack`. `publish()` stands in for the engine publishing on its own.
  - `docs/design/gui3.html`:
    - The shell runs over the transport.
    - The display reads `dev.shown`.
    - With morph off, a pinned corner selects where a save goes but never redirects an edit.
    - Corner names, the edited mark and revert come from the device.
    - The Morph page's panel: the audible-corner picker, `CornerChips`, "Save current sound to snapshot" (to the selected corner) and "Save preset to A–D", all bundle parts, with names asked through `prompt`.
    - The dock repaints when an undo or redo moves the morph toggle (a bug found by the self-check).
    - A new self-check (morph off and the toggle in history), and the determinism self-check moved onto the transport.
  - `docs/design/gui3/layout.json`: the sub's Wave override carries its reason, and the Morph page names its panel.
  - `tools/gen_gui3_controls.py`: the widget rule is `default_widget`, documented as ADR-319 item 5. An override without a reason, or a reason without an override, is red (`override-reason`), with two new plants (12 in all). `panel` is passed through.
  - `tools/labharness/gui3_seam_check.mjs`:
    - Tests now go through `SyncTransport`, plus a delayed (asynchronous) transport built in the check.
    - Four new tests: `morphoff`, `morphhistory`, `snapshots`, `sync`. That makes 15 tests and 13 plants, every plant red for its own reason (`--verbose`).
  - `verify`: the two comments updated to the new counts. No gate removed or relaxed.
  - `docs/design/gui3/README.md`: rewritten to cover the widget rule (as the rule), morph off and the snapshots, the transport with the two transports to come, and proposals 8 to 11.
  - `docs/design/gui3/shell-data.js`: regenerated. `docs/design/index.html`: regenerated; its diff is the shell's card text.

  - The determinism self-check threw because it still built the adapter on an engine instead of a transport. Fixed.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Redirecting a morph-off write aimed at a frozen corner onto the audible one:** it would let a corner revert write C's values into A. A refusal keeps B to D frozen, as ruled.
  - **Telling engine-originated changes apart by value alone:** over an asynchronous transport a stale echo looks like an engine change. The per-key send `seq` against the snapshot's `ack` tells them apart, and the test proves it on a delayed transport.
  - **A text field drawn locally for the name:** ADR-316 forbids it. The browser's `prompt` stands in, and a naming dialog is proposal 9.

The only edit after verify is this line.
- **Open questions:**
  1. "Save preset to [corner]" is read as "keep that corner's own values as its saved preset, under a name", and "Save current sound to snapshot" as "the heard sound becomes the selected corner". If the human meant loading a preset from a library into a corner, the second control changes.
  2. Saving the current sound into a frozen corner while morph is off is allowed, as an explicit save rather than an edit; to confirm.
  3. A value the adapter re-sends (on a morph move, an undo) also replaces an engine-originated value. Under host automation of a morphed parameter, whose value should win needs the morph system's ruling.
