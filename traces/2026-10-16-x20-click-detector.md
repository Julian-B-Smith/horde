# x20-click-detector — a level-relative click detector for bright tones, with its controls (numbers PROPOSED)

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B490 (acceptance 2: a detector for bright tones with a planted-step control on Crushed bells caught at 0.05 of full scale), as the first deliverable of B494 step 2 under DECISIONS ADR-225 item 12 (RULED: the method; the numbers proposed by the test author with must-fire and must-not-fire controls). Brief: the horde lead, 2026-10-16, "X20".
- **Why:** The isolated-jump detector of `tools/h2_frame_hold_check.mjs` judges a step against the render's own roughness, so on a bright patch its bar rises with the tone (B490: 0 of 10 plants up to 0.3 on Crushed bells). The new engine's acceptance tests need a detector that judges a sample against what the samples before it predict, on any render.
- **What changed:**
  1. `tools/click_detector.mjs` (new): `detectClicks(samples, sr, params) -> [{ sample, size, score }]`, plus `scores`, `eventsOf`, `warmup`, a WAV reader and a CLI. Method, per the module header: causal Butterworth band at 0.75 Nyquist; the first difference predicted; short-term LPC (autocorrelation, Levinson, white-noise correction) fitted before each block and crossfaded; a pitch predictor (33 fractional-delay taps, exact least squares) with a second pass that holds the plan after a hit; per-sample minimum of the two residuals; score against the centred 20 ms RMS without the sample's footprint and its echo, floored at -60 dB under the analysed peak; merge and echo rules. Every number is in `DEFAULTS`, marked PROPOSED.
  2. `tools/click_detector_check.mjs` (new, `WIRED:` header): `--synthetic` (judged: smooth signals read nothing; 0.05 steps caught on a sine and band-limited saws at C2 and C6; the pitch stage load-bearing; level invariance at 0.01 / 1 / 4; NaN throws) and `--bin` (judged: 0.05 steps caught 10/10 on sine C4, the engine's band-limited saw C2 and C6, Crushed bells C4; B490's 0.05 of full scale; the smooth engine set reads nothing; the band and the first difference load-bearing; level invariance. Printed: the three exceptions with their reason, spikes and corners, the naive saw, the bank and held notes by class, the old detector, the k tables).
  3. `./verify`: one block added to `fast()` (the synthetic half) and one to `full()` (the engine half, under its own `[engine gate: B490 ...]` marker). Nothing removed, skipped or relaxed; the old detector and its gate are untouched.
  4. `docs/research/2026-10-16-click-detector.md` (new): the report, tables, findings, limits, open questions.
- **Evidence consulted:** ROADMAP B486, B489, B490, B494, B460, B481; DECISIONS ADR-225 item 12 (as quoted in the brief); `tools/h2_frame_hold_check.mjs` (the old detector, imported unchanged for the comparison); `h2/harness/README.md`, `harness.mjs`, `patches.json`; `tools/h2_output_headroom_check.mjs` (the bank as the preview plays it); `tools/h2_ab.mjs` (its WAV writer, to test the CLI); `h2/engine/engine.h` (carrier shapes: `base` 2 is a naive saw, `base` 6 the capped sine-to-saw; the member's `mr` cap); `tools/test_table_check.py` (the WIRED rule).
- **Alternatives rejected (each measured, scratch programs outside the repo):** Burg fitting (residual 588 x peak on a pure tone); an unbiased-autocorrelation (Toeplitz) shortcut for the pitch taps (cancelled a reset 15 times worse); the pitch predictor on the short-term residual (block-wise filters break periodicity); predicting the sample instead of its difference (a step's lasting offset raised its own level: Crushed bells margin 1.59 against 2.95); a zero-phase FIR band (pre-ringing let the predictor anticipate the step); leaving first-pass hits out of the tap fit (the resets themselves were hits, and the fit lost them); a cascade of pitch stages for chords (no gain); a 99th-percentile local level (no gain in discrimination on bright patches, six times slower; reported as an open question); floors relative to the residual's RMS (could not quiet the band-limited saw at C1 without losing the saw at C6).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` on the staged tree; the exit code, its closing lines and the `.harness/last-verify.json` record are in the PR body and the hand-back. The engine half was run by hand on a harness binary built from this tree with the README's command (`c++ -std=c++20 -O3 -ffp-contract=off h2/harness/render_cli.cpp`): `click_detector_check: GREEN (0 row(s) failed; the engine half; ...)`, about 53 s. `./verify full` was not run end to end in this session (a from-scratch Release build of the whole tree on a machine shared with other agents); the lead's call.
- **Open questions:** (1) Every number is PROPOSED (ADR-225 item 12); k first. (2) The false-event bound is the smooth set; the bank's events are reported by class and judged nowhere: is that the bound the human wants? (3) RMS against a quantile for the local level (the brief said RMS; measured once, reported). (4) Glass horde pad steps at the engine's 32-sample tick with frameHold, stealFade and spreadRamp on (detector-free control: 89 of the 89 largest second differences on tick samples 0 to 1; gone with blade 1's depth 0, `law` 0, `kRule` 0 or `N` 1): needs its own row; the cause named in the note is a hypothesis. (5) Chords and unlocked detuned members are not seen at 0.05 (one pitch predictor); a multi-lag predictor is not built. (6) B490 acceptance (1), the dashboard hole, is not in this change. (7) The engine's band-limited saw at C1 cancels its resets well but not to zero; the cause was not found.
