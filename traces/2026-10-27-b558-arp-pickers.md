# b558-arp-pickers — picture lists for Order and Pattern, and a piano-roll mini-view in the Arp card

- **Queue item:** B558 (round 2 of B553, on the human's ruling: "In the arp bench the order and pattern selectors should also have dropdowns with visuals of the patterns. I would also in general like to see a basic arp pattern visualizer in the device for whatever is selected.").
- **Why:** the steppers are fast but blind: sixteen orders and twelve patterns are chosen by name. Clicking the value name now opens a design-system list with one picture per entry, and the card's glass shows what the current selection would play.
- **Evidence consulted:** Arp `spec/SPEC.md` 4.2 (the order table), 4.3, 4.4, 4.7, 4.8, 5.1, 5.2, 10 (read-only, Arp HEAD `de4e2af`); `docs/design/arp-face/bank.snapshot.json`; the design system's Switch, Slider and Module card guidelines (28 px rows, selection tint, the one mini-view slot); `tools/labharness/lab_wheel_scroll_check.mjs` (B301). Changed: `docs/design/arp-face-lab.html` only.
- **Alternatives rejected:** keeping the old pattern picture beside the roll (the card has one mini-view slot and the roll already shows its rests, ties, ratchets, velocity, gate and triggers; the Pattern list keeps that picture); a native `<select>` (no pictures); positioning the list inside the card (the card clips its overflow, so it is a fixed panel placed from the stepper's rectangle); a wheel listener for stepper alt+wheel (B301 allows wheel listeners through `onAltWheel` only).
- **Readings and ambiguities** (each flagged in a comment in the lab, with the SPEC section):
  - Shuffle: the Fisher-Yates index is read as `j = draw mod (i+1)`, swapping `P[i]` and `P[j]`, for `i = e-1 .. 1`; the boundary swap compares against the final `P_{c-1}`.
  - Random: epoch 0, and `kAbs` is the step number; the SPEC gives no period, the lab uses `e` as 4.7 does.
  - Stride with one held key: key + 12, from the table's own words; unreachable with the 4-key sample chord.
  - Played: needs a press order, so the sample chord is pressed G4 C4 B4 E4.
  - Not drawn in the roll: scale quantize (a degree is a semitone, as with quantize off), latch and restart, the chord stage. Probability is applied with SPEC 10's draw at the lab's seed.
- **Verify:** `nice -n 10 ./verify fast`, see the report. In headless Chrome at 980 and 600 px: no console errors, no horizontal scroll; both lists open at 36 px rows with a picture each (16 and 12 entries); one pick made one undo entry; re-picking the current entry and clicking outside made none; keyboard mode: Enter opens, arrows move, Esc closes without an entry and returns focus to the stepper, Enter picks, Cmd+Z undoes it. The lab checks itself at load: 16 of 16 orders implemented, 14 of 14 known-answer rows over the sample chord, a shuffle permutation per cycle with no repeat across 40 cycles, the roll reproducing SPEC 4.7's and 4.8's worked examples, and a must-fail control for each.
- **Open questions:** the sample chord and its press order are the lab's choice (a different chord changes every picture); the roll's window is 8 beats at least (2 bars), widening to 8 steps for slow rates.
