# b523-differential-report — the differential tool, its engine-free self-test, and the how-to

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B523 (ADR-247 audit P8; spec 10.6 item 3). The tool only: the real run waits for the new engine's bank renders, and the optional fuzz is not built.
- **Why:** the human hears a capped batch of about 20 legacy/new pairs at cutover; this ranks the 83 bank presets x 4 phrases by how different the two engines are so the batch is chosen by measurement, reported and never a gate. Four measures (BS.1770-4 loudness delta, third-octave spectral distance, envelope correlation, click delta from the ratified detector) are combined by rank-normalised sum; the rule and its reasons are in the tool's header and in every report.
- **Evidence consulted:** the brief (ROADMAP B523 and spec 10.6 item 3 quoted in it); `engine2/data/reference-renders/README.md` and `manifest.json` (file names `<preset id>__<phrase>.wav`, `source` bank/envelope); header and API of `tools/click_detector.mjs` (`detectClicks`, `readWav`, its 0.12 s unjudged warm-up); the pattern of `tools/click_detector_check.mjs` (synthetic rows, must-fail controls, mulberry32) and `tools/engine2_reference_renders.mjs`; the neighbouring `./verify` blocks (ADR-180 section 1 wiring).
- **Alternatives rejected:** weighting the four measures (a tuned number nobody has measured); counting a negative click delta (the new engine clicking less is nothing to listen for); a flat per-band spectral floor in absolute dB (a 16-bit file's noise would read as a difference between two quiet bands); re-implementing the click detector (imported instead); a self-test expectation taken from the tool's own filter output (the K-filters are checked against their analytic frequency response and the BS.1770 calibration, -3.01 LUFS for a full-scale 997 Hz sine in one channel); controls that re-run the whole disk battery (the unit controls skip the end-to-end half so the self-test stays near 4 s).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 at git 9deb37c (working tree, uncommitted), `engine2_differential --selftest: GREEN (0 row(s) failed; 23 measure rows, 9 must-fail controls)`; `test_table_check` counts the new check as wired. Self-test alone about 3.5 s. Not run: any real renders (none exist for the new engine; the 224 MB legacy asset was not downloaded, per the brief).
- **Open questions:** (1) the click detector does not judge the first 0.12 s and every phrase starts at sample 0, so an onset click of either engine is invisible to the click measure (stated in the report; a fix would be a pre-roll in the renders, a spec/phrase matter, not this tool's). (2) A pair with a render too quiet for the loudness gates cannot be level-matched, so it is listed as Unmeasured, outside the batch; the lead should look at those by hand. (3) The measures and the score are unvalidated on real renders; the first real run is the first test of whether the ranking surfaces what the human hears.
