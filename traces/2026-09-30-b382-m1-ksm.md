# b382-m1-ksm — the composed engine mirrors B150's per-rate coupling smoother (M1), plus a 48 kHz golden set

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B382 (records PR, branch `lead-records-152`), M1 and the 48 kHz golden set. The
  human's ruling on B379, 2026-09-30: M1 "Sounds good" (the composed JS mirrors B150, plus a 48 kHz
  golden set). One default-on divergence per PR (ADR-187 A1): M2 and M3 are `composed-m2-onset` and
  `composed-m3-law3`, each branched from the same two base commits.
- **Why:** SwarmSynth (`reference/swarmsaw.html`, protected) smooths the coupling targets with a literal
  0.08 per 16-sample tick, an ADR-009 per-tick constant that B150 removed from the C++
  (`src/swarm_core.h:152`, `:389-391`). The lab now plays the C++'s law. SwarmSynth is not edited: the
  engine patches the TEXT it evaluates, exactly once per anchor (`SWARM_PATCHES`), behind the flag
  `ksmPerRate` (default 1; 0 is SwarmSynth's expression).
- **What changed:**
  - Two shared base commits (13a46e3, ef1c3b9; in all three PRs, no sound change): the patch registry
    in `docs/design/scalpel-horde-engine.js`; `divergence_ledger_check` learns default-on entries
    (`default`, `ruled`, `moves`), scenarios with `over`/`sr`, L7 (the engine's SwarmSynth patches are
    the ledger's swarmsaw.html entries) and controls C6/C7; `composed_engine_check` writes the
    SwarmSynth divergences OFF (`SWARM_OFF`) in the rows that compare with SwarmSynth/DynSynth or pin a
    pre-B382 render (O1, O3, GRAV, ZERO, AA0, AA3), and can build patched references (`swarmWith`); the
    listening pass renders its committed sample on the HEARD engine (every SwarmSynth divergence off:
    the page's `heardEngine`, `listening_sample.mjs` `heard`).
  - M1: the patch (SwarmSynth's constructor gets `ksmC`, 0.08 at 44.1 kHz exactly; controlTick reads
