# b225-mixer-lab — a meter, peak hold and latching clip lamp at every stage of the chain

- **Queue item:** B225 (ROADMAP row carried in PR #729, branch `lead-records-82`).
- **Why:** The human asked for a mixer-page design lab with more visualisers and a clip warning at every step of the
  signal chain. `docs/design/mixer-lab.html` runs its own small chain sample by sample and taps 18 stages: SWARM 1/2
  gain → B209 pre-mix filter → the core's output tanh, SUB gain → filter, the mix sum, the in and out of four FX slots,
  and the master. Each tap gets a UI-Spec meter (fill, 2 px peak hold, 8 % over zone) and a CLIP lamp that latches until
  clicked. DRIVE plants a HOT gain or a one-sample SPIKE at a stage you choose. The page audits itself at load: a
  must-fire case per stage and a must-read-zero control (L0032). A single TAPS table drives both the FED/PART/OWED badges
  and the published-vs-owed table, so the two cannot disagree.
- **Evidence consulted:** ROADMAP B225 (origin/lead-records-82); `docs/design/station-page-lab.html` (model, token
  block); `src/gui/gui2.html` (:26-96/:186-212 tokens, :49 alarm, :379-383 meter CSS, :1158-1206 MIX page,
  :4989-5001 drawMeters, :5736 scope colours, :5869 spectrum caps); `docs/design-system/HORDE-UI-Spec.dc.html` (METER
  recipe; alarm "PANIC, clip, mute — NOTHING else"); `docs/design/filter-lab.html` (B209 placements, Svf transcription);
  `docs/design/fx-design-lab.html`; `src/hypersaw_clap.cpp` (applyOscGainAndMeter :2453-2488, publishViz :5079-5119,
  renderSpan :7739-7821, sub row :7836-7940, rack/matrix :8124-8145, master :8158-8190, rt coeff range :1132-1135);
  `src/gui/hypersaw_gui_common.h` (vizToValue :98-104, hzGetSpec/Scope :428-447, hzFrame :471-514);

- **Findings the table records (read from source, not measured on a build):** (1) Only three stage levels are published:
  `oscPeak[0/1]` after the tanh, where they can never exceed 1.0, and the master `outPeak`, which is |L|+|R| (6 dB hot
  for a centred signal), lands one block late and is not drawn on MIX. (2) `publishViz` runs once per block into a double
  buffer, so a once-per-frame reader never sees an over that happens in any other block. The lab models that feed:
  one-sample spikes are caught 23/23 by a sticky feed and 4/23 by the last-block feed. (3) Osc 0 is metered with
  `chunked=false`, so its peak restarts on every renderSpan call. (4) The sub, the filters, the mix and the FX slots
  publish nothing. (5) Nothing latches anywhere.
- **Alternatives rejected:** clip lamp in `--caution` as the brief offered. Rejected because gui2:49, gui2:383 and the
  UI Spec (normative for values) all reserve `--alarm` for clip. For the same reason the level fill is `--celebrate`,
  not `--meter` (the UI Spec METER recipe and gui2:381). `--meter` kept its spec role: analysers and honesty badges. A
  WebAudio-audible lab was also rejected: metering needs no sound, and a clip audition at the master is unpleasant. The
  lab stays silent and runs on a sample clock.
- **Measured (in the lab's own chain, boot patch):** audit 18/18 stages localise (HOT +18 dB, 6144 samples each);
  the control lights 0 lamps over 16 s; tightest headroom FX 2 · OUT at −3.3 dBFS, which a 30 s scratch run confirmed at
  −3.28. A 4 s control had read −4.1, i.e. 2 dB optimistic, so the control length was raised to 16 s.
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 at 5ec6063 (`.harness/last-verify.json`); `lab_load_check` GREEN, 0.27 s for this lab.
