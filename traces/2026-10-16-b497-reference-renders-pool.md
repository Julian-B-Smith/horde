# b497-reference-renders-pool — the reference renders' voice pool is 8, and the README now says so

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B497 part 2, follow-up to `traces/2026-10-16-b497-reference-renders.md` (that entry is not edited: traces are append-only).
- **Why:** the lead asked for the renders to be regenerated at `poly` 16, the new engine's default (VO-5), so that long-release presets on the trill would not steal voices. Today's engine cannot: `h2/engine/engine.h:102` has `kVoices = 8` and `poolSize()` (line 1161) returns `min(poly, kVoices)`. Rendering the trill at `poly` 8, 16 and 64 on Long tail, Tail pile-up, Glass horde pad and Breathing pad gave identical float64 digests (075e9822d2213d55, 37fb005bdff38f92, e52e22529568ba9c, 7375de43a871ed98 respectively). Regenerating would have changed a label and no sample.
- **What changed:** one paragraph in `engine2/data/reference-renders/README.md` ("The voice pool is 8"). The audio, `manifest.json`, `parameters.json` and the generator are unchanged; the zip (223636875 bytes, SHA-256 5db4dcc68d47fa62f349fd3a03ca25d5778af73a1ab1e128f9614d103132f500) stands. The lead ruled option 1 (keep `poly` 8 and the audio, document the pool); `h2/engine` is not touched.
- **Evidence consulted:** `h2/engine/engine.h` lines 102 and 1159-1162; the four-preset, three-pool trill renders above (probe run through `tools/engine2_reference_renders.mjs`'s `jobOf`, `poly` replaced).
- **Alternatives rejected:** setting `poly` 16 in the generator (a metadata claim the audio would contradict); raising `kVoices` (a change to the engine, out of scope and for the human).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast`; the exit code, closing lines and the `.harness/last-verify.json` tree check are in the hand-back.
- **Open questions:** whether the test author's judgement of the new engine against these renders should discount stealing under long releases on presets with releases beyond about 1 s; which presets steal in the renders was not measured per preset.
