# b548-fx-cream-screens — the Design System's FX modules become cream cards with a small screen each (option B); the `fx-` fills are retired for `fxs-<module>-ground|ink|mid`

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B548 (ADR-273, the human's ruling: "Let's go with option B for the FX colors"). Written by the implementer for the lead, who lands it in `traces/` (every file of the horde repo was out of scope, so this draft stays in the scratchpad). Brief origin: horde lead session, 2026-10-23.
- **Why:** the `fx-` fills (ADR-266 A1) did not fit the cream chassis. A module is now an ordinary `panel` card with an ink edge, and its identity is a small screen inset in its own display style: Shriek the 80s workstation and Sluice the Game Boy Color LCD (the two real styles), the other six labelled stand-ins. Cables, arrowheads, matrix bars and the module chips take the screen's ink. The only module colour is the screen's: three tokens per module, one value in both themes (a screen is its own display).
- **Evidence consulted:** the comparison page `fxcolour2/fx-module-colours.html` read in full (the `SK` table, `art()`, `mark()` inset, `rack`, `matrix`, `panel`, `card`, `metrics`, `Lstar`) and its trace; `40-fx.js` / `40-fx.css` before the change (`hds-src-pre-b548`); `make_tokens.py` (it reproduced the published `tokens.json` byte for byte before I touched it: `cmp`, `b548-impl/tokens-baseline.json`); `fx_*.py`, `fx_all.sh`, the eleven plants; the five components' READMEs and previews; `P/README.md`, `contrast-audit.md`, `index.d.ts`; the b547 trace and harness (the shape of this entry, the fit test).
- **What changed (files):**
  - `hds-src/js/40-fx.js`: `styleOf` now returns the cream card (`body` panel, `text` ink, `edge` ink) plus `screen: { ground, ink, mid }` from `fxs-<id>-*`, and the cable core `line` is the screen ink; new `screen()` draws the inset and its placeholder picture (ported from the page's `art()`, `pathN` and all, on tokens only); `chip()` draws a card in two layouts (a 'box': name over screen; a 'chip': name left, screen right; a lane or OUT is the name alone); the rack cards are 52 px tall (row 170-200 px, was 150-190); the matrix row headers are 78 px wide from 560 up and 34 below, and below 560 the column headers stack name over screen at 34 px tall (the top band is 40, not 28); the panel face carries name, screen (22-52 px), LED (59 px), jacks; `SCREENS` (name and stand-in flag, exported on `FxCables`) feeds the legend, whose palette rows now read "ground L* / ink on it / cord core, cased"; `contrast()` returns the screen ink on its ground and the ground's L*. Cable, matrix and sample code is otherwise unchanged (the casing was already the house ink).
  - `hds-src/css/40-fx.css`: the header comment, and `.fx-svg text.fx-l` (a name at the left of its screen; the `text-anchor:middle` rule would otherwise win over the attribute).
  - `hds/project/components/bundle.js`, `bundle.css`: rebuilt by `build.py`, never edited.
  - `hds-tools/fx_palette.py` (rewritten in place, was the `fx-` hue/lightness derivation): the eight screens' ground/ink/mid (the page's `SK` hex, unchanged), the gate (ink on ground >= 4.5:1, grounds >= 5 L* apart, L* as the page's `Lstar`), writes `fx_palette.json`; `FX_SCREEN_PLANT` proves the gate fires. `hds-tools/make_tokens.py` reads that JSON and generates the 24 `fxs-` tokens with usage notes; the 17 `fx-`, `on-fx-` and `fx-edge` tokens are gone from it. `hds/project/tokens.json` regenerated (125 colours, 209 named tokens). Run order, from the scratchpad root: `python3 hds-tools/fx_palette.py && python3 hds-tools/make_tokens.py hds/project/tokens.json && python3 hds-tools/build.py`.
  - `hds-tools/fx_contrast.py` (new assertions: every card is the panel, screens are drawn 8 / 16 / 8, screen ink 4.5:1 on its ground, grounds 5 L* apart, names in ink on the card, cords 3:1 by core or casing), `fx_all.sh` (palette gate and its two plants, four new plants); plants: `echotext` and `noedge` rewritten for the new code (same filenames), `flatfill`, `twinground`, `inkweak`, `noscreen` added. `fx_scene`, `fx_box`, `fx_sample`, `fx_reduced` needed no change.
  - The five components' `README.md` and `preview.html` (FxRack, FxMatrix, FxPanel, FxCables, ModuleCard), `P/README.md` (Colour bullet, Components paragraph), `contrast-audit.md` (the `fxs-` section replaces the `fx-` one), `components/index.d.ts` (`FxCables.SCREENS`, a comment). The FxRack, FxMatrix and FxCables `@dsCard` heights moved (450 to 470, 612 to 620, 400 to 460) because the content is taller.
- **Behaviour built, in one place:**
  - A module card is `panel` with a 1.5 px ink edge (1 px dashed asleep); the name is ink on cream (14:1 or better); the screen is the ground with a 1 px ink edge and a tiny picture in ink and mid. Asleep keeps the screen and the name at full contrast (a hollow LED and the dash are the sleep).
  - Tokens: `fxs-<module>-ground|ink|mid` for bulwark, filter, eq, drive, shriek, echo, sluice, scape, one value in both themes; each usage note says where it is used, the measured ratio, and whether the style is REAL or a STAND-IN (a placeholder until the module's skin is chosen).
  - Measured: least ink on ground 4.96:1 (Scape); least ground gap 5.38 L* (Bulwark and Shriek), both gated twice (generator and live in both themes).
  - How the cables read, both themes (documented in FxCables and `contrast-audit.md`): a screen ink is chosen for its glass, so it is a weak bare line on the well (cream well: Bulwark 1.48, EQ 1.78, Drive 1.15, Shriek 2.03, Echo 1.13, Scape 3.04; dark well: Filter 1.62, Sluice 1.47). The ink casing under every core (17.94:1 on the cream well, 16.35:1 on the dark) carries them; the `noedge` plant removes it and fails the cord floor.
  - ModuleCard: the mini-view re-points the three `scr-` roles at the module's tokens, as the Screen scheme selector does; the preview's Filter wears its stand-in Segment LCD; its ghost takes the screen's mid.
- **Alternatives rejected:** a fourth token for the page's separate cable colour (the ruling and the brief say three tokens and cables in the ink); theming the screens per theme (a screen is its own display; the page drew them the same); keeping `fx-edge` (nothing needs it: the casing is `ink`); lane and OUT chips shorter than module cards in the rack (arcs are half-ellipses with one baseline, so a shorter card would leave the arrowheads floating; all rack cards share one height); fitting name and screen side by side in a 25 px column header (they collide; stacked, taller header instead); an ink edge of `mid` on the inset as on the page (see decision 2).

## A1 (ADR-273 A1, same day): cables keep the comparison page's colours

- **Ruling:** the human answered open question 1: "Keep cables in the page's colours". Supersedes decision 1 above (cables in the screen ink) and the "three tokens" count; the brief came from the horde lead, 2026-10-23.
- **What changed:** a fourth token per module, `fxs-<module>-cable`, with the page's `SK` `c` values (bulwark `#3de8ff`, filter `#6f8a5c`, eq `#9fc4ff`, drive `#7dff9a`, shriek `#f0a830`, echo `#d9b98a`, sluice `#8bac0f`, scape `#ff4fa3`), one value in both themes. The `-ink` token note no longer claims the cables. `40-fx.js`: `styleOf().line` is the cable token (cable core, arrowhead, matrix bars and the legend all read `line`), `screen.ink` stays the picture's ink, and `contrast().screen` now measures the screen ink (it had measured `line`). Chips, names and casing unchanged. For Bulwark, EQ, Shriek and Scape the cable hex equals the ink hex; for Filter, Drive, Echo and Sluice it differs.
- **Checks:** `fx_contrast.py` keeps "cord >= 3:1 through the casing" (core OR casing, both wells and both cards, both themes) and prints the least BARE core for information: light 1.26:1 on the well (Drive), 1.21 on the panel; dark 5.05 on the well, 4.36 on the panel. New: each module's drawn core must equal its `--fxs-<module>-cable` token (a missing or wrong token fails). New plant `nocable.json` (Echo's cable token dropped) fails on that. `noedge.json` and `flatfill.json` re-anchored to the new `styleOf` line (they missed the match once the line changed; the harness said so, nothing passed silently). `noedge` now fails on the cream theme only: on dark every bare cable already reads, so the casing is load-bearing on cream (the dark run is OK by design).
