# b555-page-chrome-lab — one lab for the three pieces of chrome every page shares: the docked morph pad, the per-page modulator bank, the per-parameter morph mode

- **Queue item:** B555, B556, B557 (ADR-291; the human's ask of 2026-10-27, settled by poll: "the morph grid" is the morph pad; the menu offers Follow / Quantum / Exempt).
- **Why:** the human wants the morph pad on every page, a small bank of the modulators that move that page's parameters, and a per-parameter way to take a control out of the blend (Quantum) or out of the field (Exempt), "particularly useful for a number of arp parameters". Each row's acceptance asks for a lab the human rules on, so the lab (`docs/design/page-chrome-lab.html`) is the deliverable, with the three rules written as proposed text at its foot. No spec, no reference file and no design-system file is touched.
- **Evidence consulted:** the design system saved in the session scratchpad (README: Layout, Interaction, Morph presentation; accessibility.md; the Knob, Slider, MorphGrid, MorphInspector, KindLegend, ModuleCard, CornerChip, Switch and SteppedControl guidelines; `components/index.d.ts`); the vendored bundle `docs/design/arp-face/bundle.js` (`Session`, `Knob`, `Slider`, `XYPad`, `CornerChips`, `MorphMode`, `MorphGrid`, `SourceStrip`, `ModMatrixGrid`, `RouteList`, `H.Morph.state/engine/hear/undo`); `docs/design/arp-face-lab.html` (house lab header, undo bracketing, keyboard-mode idiom); ADR-109 and ADR-110 in DECISIONS.md (Exempt writes the live value into all four corners; the right-click became a menu); `tools/labharness/lab_wheel_scroll_check.mjs` and `lab_load_check.mjs` (what a new lab must survive).
- **What the lab shows**
  - **Dock (B555):** a left rail, 204 px, under the page tabs on every tab (Main, Osc, FX, Mod, Morph, Mix, Hist, Set), carrying the system's `XYPad`, `CornerChips` (names, edited mark, lock, revert) and `MorphMode`, plus a button to the Morph page. It collapses to a 56 px strip with the four weights; below 700 px of WINDOW width (a container query) it lies down above the page as a row. Dragging the puck morphs the page's controls live (the bundle's own render loop).
  - **Bank (B556):** `bankOf(page, mappings, depthRoutes)` is a pure function of the Session's routes and the page's own manifest keys; a depth route counts for the parameter whose depth it moves. Chips reuse the Source strip's `src-chip` class in the source-kind colour with an activity dot (size = value now, transform only). One 40 px line at the foot of every page, with "+n" past the width. A chip rings its source on the page's controls by borrowing the map-mode emphasis for the length of a render.
  - **Mode menu (B557):** right-click and the small arrow by each name open Follow / Quantum / Exempt. The override shows as a Q (staircase) or EX (slashed ring) tag, never colour alone. Knob and slider: Quantum draws the control as a pure corner (other pips vanish, tether gone, the needle steps); Exempt is the system's locked drawing (no pips, no tint, neutral rim) with "EXEMPT · held" and a dashed box. Pills: Exempt removes the owner's rule and glyph. The morph grid tags the row, a Quantum row hears its owner whatever the global mode, and exempt rows move into an "EXEMPT · held" band. A "patch state" panel lists the overrides and prints them as the preset would hold them.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - A floating or overlay pad (covers controls) and a footer pad (a pad small enough for a footer is too small to steer): the rail is the Main and Osc column the system already has, extended to every page.
  - Giving each page its own hand-kept list of modulators: the bank is derived, and a self-check moves a route and watches the bank follow.
  - Editing `bundle.js` to teach the knob about overrides: the bundle is vendored and "re-vendor, do not edit"; the lab instead overrides `Session.base` on its own instance and one-hots `S.weights` for the length of a Quantum control's render.
  - Exempt as a held value beside untouched corners: ADR-109 recorded the all-four-corners lean (nothing jumps), so the lab follows it and flags the alternative for the human.
  - Allowing Quantum on a structural parameter as a silent no-op: disabled with the reason instead (an item that cannot apply says so, ADR-110's own lesson).

- **Open questions** (for the human; also written into the lab):
  - Exempt collapses the four corners to the held value (ADR-109's lean), or holds a separate value and keeps the corners (Return to field restores the spread but the sound jumps).
  - Quantum on a structural parameter: disabled with a reason (here) or a no-op.
  - A Quantum parameter uses the field's shared quantum regions; whether it may carry boundaries of its own is the morph editor's question.
  - An Exempt parameter still takes modulation (here); whether it should.
  - The rail's width (204 px; the name column shows about 11 characters before the ellipsis) and whether it starts collapsed off Main and Osc.
  - The bundle's knob and slider drags do not write to the morph layer's undo stack (only picks, resets and pill clicks do), so "one gesture, one undo node" for a drag is the bundle's gap, not this lab's.
