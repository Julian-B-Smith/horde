# b445-os1-flip — the device default flips to os 1 (os 2 is HQ), with its ADR-197 migration check and ledger entry D5

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B445 (ADR-191), "Owed under ADR-197 (default change ⇒ migration)". Lead brief of 2026-10-08. The human's listening verdict on the os 1 / os 2 pairs, 2026-10-08, verbatim: "honestly, the difference is barely noticeable in most places. There is a very subtle difference on one note in the first example, and I think there is a slight (almost imperceptible) quality gain in the high end of a few sounds (enough to make a 2x oversampling option worth including, not enough to make it the default)".
- **Why:** the listening gate (ADR-191) passed, so os 1 becomes the default and os 2 stays as the HQ option. ADR-197 rule E: a default change carries an ADR AND a migration, so state saved before the flip keeps its sound.
- **What changed**
  1. **The flip** (the prepared patch `local/listening/b445/os1-default-flip.patch` applied cleanly to main `9243a70`): `os: 1` in `LAB_DEF` of `docs/design/scalpel-interface-lab.html`; row 88's switch labelled "2× (HQ)"; the lab's golden content blob re-pinned `6abf848e…` → `77ccee779baa09ce9fddb9d6527ecc0d68e77a8f` in `h2/README.md` and `docs/port/h2-engine.md`. The engine core's `setOS(2)`, the scenarios and both self-digests are untouched; NO digest re-pin (`h2_engine_selfdigest_check` below).
  2. **The migration check** `tools/labharness/os_default_check.mjs` (+ its frozen fixture `tools/labharness/fixtures/os-default-preflip-ported-preset.json`), wired in `./verify fast` beside the porter check, with its `PROFILES` row in `tools/labharness/sandboxed_node.mjs` (the sandbox launcher refuses a script with no row). An addition under ADR-180 §1; nothing removed or relaxed.
  3. **The ledgered divergence D5** in `docs/port/divergences.json` (D4's shape: `status: planned`, `flag: null`, `owner: B445`, plus `landed` and `ruled`). The ledger's vocabulary is `built` (an engine flag) or `planned`; a lab-default divergence has no flag, so `planned` is the only status it can carry and the entry says the divergence is in force. `js_limit: null`: the divergence is a patch default, so nothing is out of the browser's reach.
  4. `docs/port/cpu-ledger.md`: the one sentence "The default does not flip until the human has listened" is now past tense and points here. It states no other default.
- **Where saved state lives, and how each format is migrated (evidence: grep of the lab and of `tools/`, `docs/design/`, `src/`)**
  - **The lab writes no patch state.** The only storage is `localStorage` under `LOGO_STYLE_KEY` (the logo style). There is no download, clipboard, `sessionStorage`, `indexedDB`, `history` or hash write; the one `createObjectURL` is the audio worklet. A preset loads only from `?preset=<name>` (a name), `reference/scalpel/data/presets.json` (protected, 83 presets, none carries `os`) and the lab's `ENV_PRESETS` (12, none carries `os`). No corner or bank format is written. `os_default_check` T5 holds this and fails if a patch-state surface appears.
  - **The only pre-flip artifact that can be reloaded is the porter's output** (`tools/port_legacy_presets.mjs` → `local/legacy-presets/legacy-presets.json`, git-ignored). Its records carry `os` on EVERY preset: `ctx.defaults` has it, and ACCOUNTING row G5 sets it from the legacy Oversample (absent or off → 1, on → 2). The marker is therefore the record's own `os` field, and `applyPreset`'s `Object.assign({}, DEF, pr.params, …)` already lets it win over DEF. **No code in the lab or porter had to change to migrate it.** (A first draft assumed the porter writes `os: 2` for an absent G5; running it showed it writes `os: 1`, since the legacy default is OFF. Ported presets were already at 1, so they are unaffected by the flip, and one whose legacy Oversample was on carries `os: 2`.)
  - **Legacy sessions** (the legacy plugin's own state, `src/hypersaw_clap.cpp` stateJson, `bank_check`, the factory bank) belong to the old plugin and are not read by the composed engine (ADR-197: "They can stay on the old system"). Not touched.
  - **horde 2's preset format does not exist yet (B395).** No premise of the brief ("a saved state with no `os` field") has an instance in the repo. When B395 defines the format it MUST write `os` explicitly or carry a format version; T2/T3 pin the rule it has to meet. This is the open item for the lead.
  - **Factory and bench presets** (`presets.json`, `ENV_PRESETS`) carry no `os` and play at the new default, os 1. That is the intended product behaviour, and the listening gate covered it. Not edited.
- **The check and its control**
  - `T1` `LAB_DEF.os` is 1 (read from the literal, comments stripped, composed the way the lab composes DEF, anchored) and the oracle/engine default stays 2. `T2` the frozen pre-flip fixture (a ported preset whose legacy Oversample was on, `os: 2`) loads at os 2. `T3` a record with no `os` (inline, and bench preset 1) loads at os 1. `T4` the live porter writes `os` on every record: Oversample absent / off / on → 1 / 1 / 2. `T5` the persistence tripwire above.
