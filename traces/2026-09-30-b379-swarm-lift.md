# b379-swarm-lift — Swarm Core lifted into h2/cores/swarm, byte-identical plus a four-line ledger

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B379 (B332 phase 1b), narrowed by the lead on 2026-09-30 to step 1 only, after the implementer's grounded refusal of steps 3–5.
- **Why:** The human approved the lift under the B378 audit's advice. A byte-identical lift carries the legacy parity proof (ADR-187 L3), so this PR makes the copy provably the legacy code: every difference is ledgered and checked. It also records why the composed layer cannot be built yet. The contraction rule's "pending" labels come off because the human ruled that the shipped build is the tested build.

- **What was measured (scratch, this Mac, 2026-09-30):**
  - **E1 codegen.** An `-O3 -ffp-contract=off` object of a TU calling the lifted core's `setParam`, `noteOn` and `render` is byte-identical with and without the forwarder: sha256 `b83d8659…0ac99` both.
  - **M1 and M2** (lifted core against SwarmSynth, one note, 1 s): bit-exact at 44.1 kHz for K 0, K 0.35 and onset +0.5.
    - At 48 kHz, K 0.35 gives rms 1.44e-4 (max 2.42e-3) and onset +0.5 gives rms 9.7e-4. Both come from B150's rate-dependent ksmC, 0.0737 at 48 kHz, against SwarmSynth's 0.08.
    - Onset −0.5 gives rms 1.16e-1 at both rates (ADR-056's bipolar onset against SwarmSynth's `8·o²`).
  - Details and method: `docs/port/phase-1b.md`.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Namespace `horde2::swarm` with the qualified references rewritten.** That is 12 more changed lines. The nested `horde2::swarm::hypersaw` keeps each file's diff to its one namespace line, and lookup resolves `hypersaw::GlideCore` and `forcecore::` to the copies.
  - **Making `controlTick` public by moving an access specifier.** That would also expose `sr` and `ksmC`; a one-line forwarder exposes only the tick.
  - **A composed layer over `razor_core.h` through the `render<T>` explicit-specialisation backdoor, or a copied blade path.** Either duplicates the blade path out of the 1a harness's reach. Not built; it is a decision for the human (phase-1b.md).
  - **A check that includes both `src/` and `h2/` to compare renders.** Legal with distinct namespaces, but it blurs ADR-186's boundary. The byte-identity check plus the E1 behaviour check prove the same thing without it.
