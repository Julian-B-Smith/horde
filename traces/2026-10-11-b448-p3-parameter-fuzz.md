# b448-p3-parameter-fuzz — the parameter fuzz of the horde 2 engine, its planted-fault controls and its nightly entry

- **Queue item:** B448 (Blind-Spot Armor), phase 2 Wave 1, package P3, ruled in ADR-209 and ADR-211. Proposal: `docs/strategy/blind-spot-armor-phase2.md` section 1 rows R6 and R3, section 3 row P3.
- **Why:** row R6's exit condition asks for "a seeded fuzz [that] reaches every parameter key of each enrolled engine (counted), with sweeps and stacked changes, asserting finite output, a bounded pre-limiter level and silence after release". Before this nothing drove the C++ engine across its declared parameter space; the JS gauntlet measures and does not gate C++.
- **What changed:**
  - `tools/h2_fuzz_check.mjs` (new, `WIRED: ./verify full`): cases made from the declared table (`tools/patchspace/dependency_tree.json`), rendered through `h2_harness_render`, every sample judged. Static, sweep and stacked-change cases; a preamble that reaches every extreme by construction; hits per key counted from the commands sent; failures minimised (ddmin, 1-minimal) and printed as one replay line; kept cases replayed first; `--deep` prints `KIT-GATE cases=<n>`; `--measure` prints the numbers the thresholds are built from.
  - `h2/harness/control_fuzz.h` (new) and three rows in `h2/harness/adapters.h`: `control/fuzz-blowup`, `control/fuzz-nan`, `control/fuzz-hang`, each one planted fault in one corner of the same table.
  - `tools/h2_fuzz_kept.json` (new, empty): the kept failing cases. One exclusion line for it in `tools/golden_registry_check.py` (inputs, no stored result).
  - `tools/nightly/jobs.json`: one job, `fuzz-h2-engines`, floor 20000.
  - `verify`: one line under `# [harness gate: P3 parameter fuzz]`, as the brief gave it.
- **Three things a reader must know before trusting it:**
  1. **The sampler is not imported; it is restated over the same table.** `tools/patchspace/space.mjs` and `gauntlet.mjs` start with the lab sandbox guard, which refuses an entry script with no profile row and forbids child processes; this tool must start the render binary (P2's trace records the same wall). Two more facts made a restatement the better tool anyway. Measured: the lab's position-to-value map gives 0 for the declared maximum 1 of `c` and `c2` (frac(1)), and 7.940000000000001 for `dissolve`'s declared maximum 7.94; the other 71 continuous rows land exactly. And the gauntlet resets every key the dependency tree calls inactive to its default, which for a robustness fuzz is a narrowed range. Here an end is the table's own number and every key keeps its draw.
  2. **Chords share one note's velocity budget, and that was decided after seeing a measurement.** With full velocities on 1 to 4 notes, 12000 cases (seeds 46152 and 20261011) had worst bus peaks of 17.19 and 9.15, nothing on the rail, nothing non-finite. The 17.19 case minimises to eleven items (nine swarm members at zero detune, blade 2 on, gain stepped to 1 under four notes; 15.67). Held for 10 s that minimised case puts 2 samples ON the rail. Held for 40 s at one sixteenth of the gain it reads a steady 0.6 with a burst to about 1.2 every 5.3 s (peak 1.27): about 10 and 20 at full gain. So it is bounded and periodic, not growing, and it is above what the adapter can see. A fuzz that calls every rail reading a blow-up would report that stable chord as one, and no margin fits between 17.19 and 19. The budget (each velocity is a drawn 0.1..1 divided by the note count) keeps the judged level near one note's. **This removes full-velocity chords from the tool's sight. It is the lead's and the human's to accept or replace.** The alternative is in the tool's header: replay a rail case with its gain divided down, since the engine's last line is linear in gain (shown by hand above), which would let the tool read levels above the rail. It changes what "on the rail" means, so it was not built.
  3. **21 engine keys are not fuzzed.** The table lists them as fixed and declares no range (`bend`, `poly`, `dist`, `seed`, `bpm`, `beatMult`, `driftMode`, `motionCenter`, `freqGlide`, `keepPhase`, `pivotMode`, `grav`, `basin`, `onsetScatter`, `onsetAlpha`, `attackScatter`, `voiceEnv`, `relScatter`, `aaCarrier`, `aaXin`, `aaLoop`). The tool reads the engine's own key list from `h2/engine/engine.h`, compares it with the table and prints these as a HOLE on every run. `kCustom` (a string row with one declared value) is set and never varied. Row R6 says "every parameter key"; this run reaches the 112 keys that have a declared range.
- **Thresholds: PROPOSED, not ratified (ADR-209 item 5).** One table at the top of the tool. Measured 2026-10-11 with `node tools/h2_fuzz_check.mjs --measure --cases 6000` at seeds 46152 and 20261011: 12000 cases, 7,968,665,758 samples, nothing on the rail, nothing non-finite.

  | name | measured worst | margin | value | band |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `prePass` | bus peak 4.79601 (seed 20261011, case 5024; the other seed read 4.62679) | x2 (6 dB) | 9.6 | at or under: pass; above and under the rail: grey |
  | `preFail` | not this tool's: the adapter's limit of sight (`h2/harness/README.md`: 19 is under it, 19.2 on it) | none | the rail | the bus reads infinity: fail |
  | `settlePass` | 0.1823 s from the release law's end to under the floor (seed 20261011, case 2343; the other seed read 0.1808) | x1.5 | 0.274 s | silent by then: pass |
  | `settleFail` | the same | x3 | 0.547 s | silent only by then: grey; still above the floor: fail |
  | `silentFloor` | not measured here: the line `tools/patchspace/metrics.mjs` `silence()` already draws | none | -90 dBFS | the two settle rows are measured against it |

  The margins are argued, not measured, and are the human's to change. Non-finite samples and unknown keys have no band.
- **Evidence (all measured 2026-10-11, darwin-arm64, Apple clang, Node 24, the Release `h2_harness_render`; `<s>` is this session's scratch directory, nothing from it is committed):**
  - The PR-size run, `node tools/h2_fuzz_check.mjs --bin build-release/h2_harness_render`: GREEN, exit 0. 1020 cases of `composed` (348 static, 336 sweeps, 336 stacked changes), 193,411,190 samples judged, 0 non-finite, 0 on the rail, worst bus peak 2.5216 (case 750), silence judged in 572 cases including the longest release (11.51 s), worst tail peak 4.68e-8.
  - Keys: 113 rows (112 engine keys and the mapped `b1on`), all set. Lowest five by set commands: `kCustom` 1020, `mUnit` 1045, `b2order` 1050, `glideAlways` 1051, `cScale` 1051. All 74 continuous keys were sent their declared minimum (fewest: `m2`, 223 cases) and maximum (fewest: `I`, 225 cases); 141 of 141 options of 38 option lists were sent (fewest: `kRule2=5`, 78 cases); 112 of 112 keys swept and 112 of 112 led a stacked change.
  - Controls, same 1020 cases: `control/fuzz-blowup` (fb = 1, K = -1) found in 102 cases, exactly the 102 that enter the corner, first at case 2, minimised to `fb = 1, K = -1`; `control/fuzz-nan` (detune = 100, S = 0, aa = 0) found in 49, exactly the 49, first at case 7, minimised to those three; `control/fuzz-hang` (R = 10, A = 2000) found in 71, exactly the 71, first at case 8, minimised to those two; `control/tone` reported nothing; an undeclared key was reported; the key report with one key's counts removed went red five ways.
  - The red and grey paths on the REAL engine, shown with a bound tightened in memory only (a scratch script that sets the exported `THRESHOLDS` and calls `main`; nothing committed): `prePass` 2.0 gave 3 grey cases, minimised to `gain = 0.886`, `gain = 1`, and `mirror = 3, gain = 0.925`, one note each; `settlePass` 0.02 and `settleFail` 0.06 gave 11 failing cases, exit 1, the first three minimised (for example `xm = 1, R = 10`) with their replay and KEEP lines.
  - A replay line of the NaN plant: `NON-FINITE; 30 non-finite output samples`, exit 1. The same three sets on `composed`: `clean`, exit 0.
  - Determinism: `--procs 1` against `--procs 4` over 348 cases, outputs identical but for the first line, which states the process count. Two PR-size runs gave the same output.
  - Sizes: `--cases 100` is refused (exit 2: "100 cases cannot reach every key of composed: its table needs at least 348"). `--deep --cases 1020` ends with `KIT-GATE cases=1020`. Through `tools/nightly/run.py` with a scratch job list: a job at its floor of 1020 read `ran 1020`; one at 348 under the same floor read `failed: below floor: judged 348, floor 1020`. The 20000-case deep run itself was NOT run here (the brief forbids it).
  - Run time: the PR-size run took 27 s twice on a nearly idle machine (87 and 89 s of user CPU, 4 render processes), and an earlier build of the tool 1 min 47 s over its 1020 cases at a load average of about 40. At 26.5 ms a case, 20000 cases are about 9 minutes here. The hosted runner's speed is not measured; four to six times slower is an assumption, giving 35 to 53 minutes.
  - `node h2/harness/selftest.mjs`: GREEN, 30 rows. `python3 tools/engine_enrolment_check.py`: GREEN with the three new control rows in the table.
  - The first 6000 cases of the deep run (same seed, same cases) were read by the 6000-case measurement: nothing on the rail, nothing non-finite, bus peak 4.63 against a pass line of 9.6. Cases 6000 to 19999 are unread.
- **Observed, not judged:**
  - After the voices free, the output does not reach exact zero for a long time: it rings down at the DC blocker's rate (measured in one case of an earlier build: 1e-3 at 0.075 s, 1e-6 at 0.21 s, 1e-9 at 0.35 s past the release law, and 1e-35 at 1.5 s). At that slope it reaches subnormal numbers about 14 s after the voices free (argued from the slope, not measured). ADR-211 rules flush-to-zero on in the shell.
  - `tools/test_table_check.py` reads `tools/*_check.cpp`, `tools/*_check.py` and `tools/labharness/*_check.mjs`. It does not read `tools/h2_fuzz_check.mjs`, so a wrong wiring claim in this file's header would not turn it red. `./verify fast` was green before the `verify` line was added.

- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - A sandboxed helper that imports the gauntlet's sampler, with a profile row in `tools/labharness/sandboxed_node.mjs`: outside this package's files, and the sampler it would import has the three faults in point 1 for this use.
  - A numeric fail line for the level (for example four times the measured worst): 19.2 at these numbers, which is the rail. The rail is the one line the adapter really has.
  - Two notes at most instead of a velocity budget: the same problem at half the size, and less polyphony.
  - Reading above the rail by replaying a case at reduced gain: shown by hand to work, not built, because it redefines "on the rail" (point 2).
  - Varying the nightly's seed from the run number: more search per night, but then a red night is not reproducible from the repo alone unless the seed is read from the receipt. Left as a question; `--seed` exists.
  - Editing `h2/harness/README.md` to list `control_fuzz.h`: not among this package's files. The row is in the hand-back for the lead.
