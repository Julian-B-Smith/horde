# b547-chain-line-gains — the Design System's Chain line now sets the stage gains: drag a dot, one gain model with Gain staging

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B547 (ADR-272 ruling 2; the human asked "Would it be plausible to also control the levels of the gain stages from the horizontal viewer?" and ruled "Yes, drag a dot"). Written by the implementer for the lead, who lands it in `traces/` (every file of the horde repo was out of scope, so this draft stays in the scratchpad).
- **Why:** the line (B545) was a reading; the human wants it to be a control too. A dot is now two things: at rest the tap's measured peak hold, and while held the stage's gain handle with a dB readout beside it, settling back to the peak hold on release. The line holds no gain of its own: `ChainLine` takes `gain(id)` and `onGain(id, dB)` (the consumer's one model, the one Gain staging's faders also read and write), reads `gain(id)` on every paint and writes only through `onGain`, so a fader moves the line and the line moves a fader. The ranges and defaults are the stage list's own (`stage.gain`), which Gain staging's faders use, so both views move one value. Gain staging's code did not change: its existing `gain(id)` / `setGain(id, db)` / `onGain` were enough.
- **Evidence consulted:** the B545 trace and `60-chain-blend.js` / `.css` (the line); `50-ring-gain.js` (the fader's drag law :211-214 `d / DRAG * (shift ? .25 : 1)`, the key step :219, `setGain`/`gain` :297-299, `chain()` ranges :169-188); `P/README.md` Interaction (160 px, shift ×0.25, relative, dblclick resets); `RingXY` (:98-135, the touch-action and hand + offset pattern); `00-core.js` `S.reduce` (reduced-motion idiom); the b544/b545 harnesses and tests (copied and adapted into `b547-impl/`).
- **Behaviour built (all in `60-chain-blend.js`/`.css`):**
  - Drag: relative, vertical only, 160 px = the stage's whole gain range, shift ×0.25 (incremental, so shift mid-drag does not jump), an unclamped hand so the value cannot drift from the pointer. Double-click sets `stage.gain.def`. ↑ ↓ on the probed dot (← → pick it): 1 % of the range, shift 0.25 %; `keys` gates them (default on, unlike Gain staging's default off, because the line's ← → already work without an option; ↑ ↓ with nothing pointed at are left to the page).
  - Held: the dot is filled `scr-value`, moves by the dB the gain has moved since the grab (the peak will land there), with a boxed readout ("+6.0 dB", the stage's own decimals and `−∞` floor) beside it, flipped to the left near the right edge. Released: `off` (handle minus measured) decays with a 0.11 s time constant on the `dt` passed to `step` (Gain staging's bar release); with reduced motion it is zero at once.
  - Fan: a column holding more than one dot (the source lanes) spreads sideways 28 px apart, each at its own level and named on a ground backing, while the pointer is over the box round the column and its fan (so leaving the stack's own spot does not close it under the hand), or while the keys have one of them, or while one is held. Touch: the first press on a stack fans it and grabs nothing; the next press grabs; the fan closes at a press outside it (a touch `pointerleave` is ignored, or the lift would close it).
  - No gain, not draggable: a stage whose `gain` is null (or a line built without `gain`/`onGain`) only names; the readout says "no gain control". The limiter's gain reduction is not on the line at all (B545), so the case is carried by the generic rule and tested with a stage list whose `f1o` has no gain.
  - `touch-action: pan-x` on the glass (a vertical drag is the gain). Grab radius 16 px (a 32 px target); fanned dots 28 px apart (28 px targets); readout, aria-label and the key swatch ("held: the gain handle") updated.
- **Alternatives rejected:** a second gain value inside the line (two models drift); changing Gain staging to publish its gains through a shared object (its `gain`/`setGain` already are the seam, so the part stays byte-identical, checked with `cmp`); the handle riding the pointer 1:1 (then shift ×0.25 would make the dot disagree with the number; riding the dB the gain moved keeps one meaning); a vertical fan (it would break the y = level reading, a horizontal fan keeps it); opening the fan on focus alone (a mouse click focuses the canvas and would pin the fan open; only keyboard focus-visible or an arrow key opens it); `touch-action: none` (the whole glass would stop scrolling the page); making the dot a real focusable element per tap (17 tab stops, and the line is one canvas by design; ← → already walk it).

- **Open questions:** (1) "its faders move the line's handles": at rest a dot has no handle, it is the reading, so a fader moves the line by moving the level (the dot lands on it) and the readout's gain; no separate gain tick is drawn. If the human wants a persistent gain mark on the line, that is a design change. (2) Fanned source dots lie over the next columns' dots for as long as the fan is open (at 360 px they cover F1 IN and F1 OUT); the nearest dot wins a press and the names sit on a ground backing, but a fanned name can touch a neighbour's. A fan to the left or above would need room the canvas does not have. (3) `touch-action: pan-x` means the page cannot be scrolled by a vertical swipe that starts on the glass; the card's margins and the controls above and below still scroll. (4) The y range is still the lab's −48..+6, so a handle dragged past it sits on an edge while the gain keeps moving (the readout carries the number); a ruling on the mixer panel's range (B402) settles it. (5) The `@ds-bundle` list in `00-core.js` and `design-system.json` still name neither the Chain line nor Gain staging (B545 open question 5); out of scope here. (6) The fader's own `keys` default is off while the line's gain keys default on; the lead may prefer one rule. (7) `index.d.ts` was not type-checked (no `tsc` on this machine); the added members are optional and follow the file's own idiom.
