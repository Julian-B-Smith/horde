# b441-e1-selfdigest — the composed engine's C++-vs-C++ bit-identity digest, and a libm call-site count

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B441 phase 2, the step after E0 ("a C++-vs-C++ bit-identity digest of all 543 scenarios, with a 1-ULP must-fail control and a libm-call count per candidate, because the parity gate alone cannot prove a change output-neutral"; the row as recorded on `lead-records-183`, `cfb84c9`, not yet on main). Dispatched by the lead as E1.
- **Why:** C1–C3 must prove they change no output bit (ADR-187 item 5: output-neutral work leaves every digest unchanged). The parity gate (rms and max-abs < 1e-6 against the JS, a 30 % mean bit-exact floor) passes a 1e-12 move, so it cannot.
- **What:** `tools/h2_engine_selfdigest_check.cpp` replays the parity stream's 543 scripts through the parity build (-O2 -ffp-contract=off, H2_ENGINE_FAULTS, fault 0) and hashes each scenario's samples (raw double bits), events (EventLog counts and its two order-sensitive hashes) and readouts with FNV-1a 64; one line per scenario plus a TOTAL; compared with the committed `h2/engine/selfdigest.darwin-arm64.appleclang-16.node24.txt` (keyed like the floor; unkeyed: printed, SKIP, never a pass; same platform with another key: a loud WARNING). Controls through the same verdict function: ULP (one sample of `T/mode 0 :: chord` nudged one ULP: exactly that row red) and FAULT (K1, fault 9: 474 of 543 rows red, bar 100). `--repin` writes the reference only from a full stream with both controls fired; the header limits it to a ledgered output-changing divergence. Wired into `./verify full` after the FMA control, on the stream the parity check already read (no second JS render). `tools/h2_libm_count.py` (UNWIRED, context) counts libm call sites in the emitted assembly of a replay() TU at the product flags (-O3 -DNDEBUG -ffp-contract=off) and the parity flags (-O2 after -O3, H2_ENGINE_FAULTS), written to `h2/engine/libm-calls.darwin-arm64.appleclang-16.txt`. h2/README.md gains a paragraph.
- **Evidence consulted:** `tools/h2_engine_parity_check.cpp` (floor keying, `--det-digest`, kControls), `tools/h2_engine_stream.h` (replay), `h2/engine/engine.h` :73–93 (fault hooks; fault 9 = `field.faultKsm(0.08)`), `h2/engine/js.h` :90 (EventLog), `CMakeLists.txt` h2 targets and `build-release/.../flags.make` (actual flag order), `tools/h2_rules_check.py` (every h2 target needs -ffp-contract=off), `tools/test_table_check.py` (WIRED/UNWIRED grammar), DECISIONS ADR-187 items 5 and 7, h2/README.md.
- **Measured:** the check alone 23.4 s wall here (the clean replay plus the K1 replay, single-threaded; the parity check is 17.1 s and the shared JS render 29.5 s). Tampering one reference line turned it red naming that scenario (exit 1), restored. libm call sites, product / parity: __sincos_stret 7/5, asin 2/2, atan2 3/3, cos 23/21, exp 23/22, exp2 27/26, fmod 15/7, ldexp 1/1, log 13/11, log2 15/3, nan 108/106, pow 9/9, sin 23/20, tan 1/1, tanh 2/2; TOTAL 272/239. `nan` is `std::nan` called as a function (108 sites), context for the audit's NaN-checking min/max.
- **Alternatives rejected:** digesting inside h2_engine_parity_check (reuses its C++ render, saves ~11 s, but entangles a new gate with a protected one and its controls); folding each event into the FNV directly (needs an EventLog edit in `h2/engine/js.h`, an engine change that would collide with #931); multithreading the replay (saves ~20 s, but adds Threads linkage across three CI platforms for a 23 s check); a new engine fault for FAULT (K1 already IS a planted engine constant).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 (pre-commit tree); `./verify full` exit 0 at `2f4e18f` (`{"target":"full","exit":0,"git":"2f4e18f","ts":"2026-10-04T07:27:03Z"}`, 532 s wall), selfdigest GREEN 543/543 with both controls PASS. Both re-run on the head that adds this trace; results in the PR.
