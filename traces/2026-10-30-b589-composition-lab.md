# b589-composition-lab — a variant sheet for the tiny tier, clusters, dense-page paradigms and the modulators' views

- **Queue item:** B589 (the design-system round for GUI 3), with B594 (the modulators), under ADR-334 (horde #1294, not yet on main when this branch was cut from `3142e8c`).
- **Why:** the human, 2026-10-30: the mod page and the bank "need a lot of work"; modulators need visuals and "quite a bit more compact" controls; "a fresh set of tiny knobs"; minimal views ("as much as a visual readout and a map button"); unmapped modulators removable; and "a suitable design paradigm to handle large numbers of controls in a tight space". ADR-333 had already asked for compact variants of knobs, sliders, buttons and drop-downs for clustered sections, as a variant sheet.
- **What changed:**
  - `docs/design/composition-lab.html` (new): a single-page variant sheet. A "How to answer" panel (every id with its trade-off, generated from what the page builds; ten open questions, each with a recommendation; what happens next per ADR-316). T: tiny knobs T1 (16 px), T2 (20), T3 (22), T4 (16 px level cell), against the bundle's utility and compact knobs (T0, the control); the tiny slider TS1 and value cell TS2; two companion sets TC1 (house boxes at 16 px) and TC2 (flat). C: ENV 2's ten rows as C1 (one row, shared label line), C2 (stages × time/curve matrix), C3 (docked to the picture), C4 (value cells), against the shell's compact section (C0), plus the proposed cluster tokens and rules. P: Osc 1's 126 controls in the shell's page area as P1 (focus + lens), P2 (progressive disclosure), P3 (a parameter table), P4 (macro-first tiles). M: eight modulator kinds with live pictures in a compact card, a thin row and a micro chip; the bundle's full editors; the bank as M1 (compact cards, ruled form), M2 (thin rows), M3 (chips); removal M-R1 / M-R2 (marked as the human's open ruling); the Mod page as M4 (card grid + focus) and M5 (list + detail).
  - `docs/design/index.html`: regenerated by `tools/gen_lab_index.py` (the lab's `lab-part` Presentation and `lab-review` B589 tags), not hand-edited.
  - Nothing in `docs/design/arp-face/`, `docs/design/gui3*`, `docs/design-system/`, `specs/`, `reference/`, ROADMAP or DECISIONS.

Interaction probe: Map on a thin row then a click on a tiny knob adds one route and clears the pending flag; × under M-R2 removes ENV 2 and its two routes, and Undo restores both; a 40 px drag on a tiny knob moves its editing corner by 0.25 (the 160 px law).
- **Checks run:** `tools/labharness/lab_load_check.mjs` on the lab (GREEN); `lab_wheel_scroll_check.mjs` (GREEN: the lab registers no wheel listener, so it needs no `onAltWheel` entry; the tiny controls take shift-drag and arrows instead); a search of the lab for `Math.random` and clock reads (only the header comment saying there are none).
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Editing the bundle or the shell to show the tiny tier:** ADR-316; proposals are drawn in the lab and marked PROPOSED.
  - **A copied snapshot of the parameter rows:** the lab loads `gui3/shell-data.js` itself, so it cannot drift from the shell.
  - **Calling `Viz.kit` for every small modulator picture each frame:** it reads computed style per call; the small pictures use one cached palette of the same screen roles, and the bundle painters draw only on change.
  - **An alt+wheel on the tiny controls:** it would need a new `onAltWheel` copy and an inventory entry in the wheel check; it comes with the bundle's gesture once the tier is drawn there.
