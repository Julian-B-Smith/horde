# b316-patchspace-p1-p3 — dependency tree, coherence metrics and the gauntlet for the composed engine

- **Queue item:** B316, phases P1–P3 only. I read the row verbatim from `origin/lead-records-122:ROADMAP.md` (records PR #810), beside B199, B275, B276 and B313. P4 (fit distributions) and P5 (the lab's RANDOM button and FUZZ mode) are not built.
- **Why:** The human asked for a random-patch button bounded by statistical distributions, informed by a dependency tree ("no need generating all the parameters for a blade that's turned off"). The lead's plan builds it in stages: the tree as saved DATA (for morph, the mod matrix and B275's "active when" field), a validated metric suite, then a seeded gauntlet over the engine.

### Shared module: `space.mjs`

- Loads the composed engine the way `composed_engine_check.mjs` does.
- Takes the parameter table and tapers from the SCALPEL lab itself. The `R(...)` rows and `toPos`/`fromPos` are sliced out and evaluated; a missing anchor throws.
- Takes defaults from an engine instance.
- Implements the JSON condition grammar and its evaluator.
- Scope: 113 patch parameters. It names 8 excluded rows (pitch, tab power, x rows, master) and 9 unsampled engine keys.

### P1: `gen_dependency_tree.mjs` → `dependency_tree.json` (181 kB)

**Static layer.** Every parameter has an `active_when` condition: its ROLE. There are 24 named predicates, for example "blade 1 sounds", "the upper blade reads collision pitch" and "a numeric per-cycle DC estimate". Every condition and predicate carries the engine guard text it rests on, and the generator resolves each one to file:line.

**Declared side channels.** Twelve `SC_*` predicates name the OTHER engine paths through which a parameter is also heard. Every one of them was confirmed by the probe.

**Structured layer.** The lab's greying rules are executed, not paraphrased: `inertWhy`, `HORDE_INERT`, `RULE_OWN`, the `FOLLOW` table with `linked()`, and `isFM`. The three greying expressions in the lab's view code are pinned by their text. On the brief's pointer: ACCOUNTING §6 is the structured-discussion *questions*. The greying the human actually sees lives in the lab, so the lab is what is parsed. This is stated in the generator's header.

**Empirical probe.**
- 482 seeded contexts: 24 shared, plus targeted ones per parameter (2 where the role is true, 2 where it is false) and per side channel.
- 3170 comparisons. A parameter counts as LIVE if any alternative value changes the float32 output by more than 1e-6.
- The script is 3 notes: overlapping, then a note during release, at velocity 0.8. This makes mono/legato glide, "glide always" and velocity rows visible.
- Attacks are drawn under 20 ms so Decay and Sustain are audible within the window.

**Result.**
- 0 STRUCTURAL disagreements: nothing is heard outside its role plus its side channels, and no role is declared live without ever being heard.
- 11 REGIME rows: the role holds, but that context's values leave nothing to hear. Examples: blades that never overlap for colK/colB; a narrow blade collapsed under the bypass for mirror; a Hz cut-rate so low the square blade never flips for kRule2; a jump-free patch for aa. They are reported per parameter.
- 46 side-channel hits.

**Output properties.**
- The JSON is independent of worker count: 3 and 6 workers produce byte-identical output.
- It holds counts and decade exponents only, never raw float deltas.

**Findings the probe surfaced** (these are engine or oracle facts for the lead; evidence is in the JSON):
1. **SC_DCRES.** The modulator rate (m/mHz/mUnit, their twins, and b2fm) is heard in NON-FM blades. The per-cycle DC estimate's resolution reads it unconditionally: `feats += s.mEff*w`, razor-core.js:577. This moved the output by up to 0.06 in a Crush context. It is probably an oracle defect.
2. **SC_SCANCOL.** Collision → pitch is heard by an upper Crush blade with hard steps. The PolyBLEP scanner places Crush step corrections using the collision accumulator (razor-core.js:633), which the Crush voice never reads.
3. **Reflected Crush jumps at the blade exit at any slew.** At the exit, hp returns to the hold level that slews from the blade START's base value. The slew-to-base lands at st + w/2. Found as `aa` heard in a patch with no other jump.
4. **SC_DRAWS.** The drift spread law's per-tick `Math.random` draws shift the ONE shared stream, and Noise values and FM-free `modX` both read that stream.
5. **SC_DCPAIR.** Upper hears > 0 switches per-cycle DC to the numeric pair estimate even when only one blade sounds. The blocker family (xm/fb/colK/colB/b2on) switches an 8 Hz HP in.
6. **cScale is never heard in the composed engine.** `keff()` feeds only increments that are overwritten, plus the skipped `settle()`.
7. **Lab greys-but-live** (9 groups):
   - Blade 1's row is drawn "off" while blade 2 follows its spreads, rotation and mirror. bspread, kspread, kRuleAmt, rotRate and mirror are heard through their role.
   - The FM group is ghosted while m/mHz (and the blade-2 twins) are heard through SC_DCRES.

### P2: `metrics.mjs` + `metrics_check.mjs` (WIRED: `./verify fast`, ~4 s)

The metrics are aliasing, flatness, root presence, Sethares roughness, DC, level/crest/LUFS-like, silence, clicks, non-finite, and CPU. They are measurements, not gates. The methods are stated in the header.

Validation on constructed signals, each with a zero and a high control:

| metric | must read ~zero | must read high |
|---|---|---|
| nonFinite | clean sine: 0 | planted: 5 |
| dc | sine: 1e-17 | sine + 0.1: 0.1 |
| LUFS | 997 Hz full-scale stereo: −0.0001 (target 0) | |
| crest | sine: 3.01 dB | |
| clicks | sine, band-limited saw and naive saw: 0 | one step: 2 frames at 36 dB |
| flatness | sine: 8e-12 | white noise: 0.967 |
| root | a tritone up: 2e-6 at Δ6 | sine and saw at the note: 1; missing fundamental: 1 |
| roughness | one sine: exactly 0; fifth: 0.00068 | minor second: 0.090 (131× the fifth) |
| aliasing | band-limited square vs itself: −120 | naive square at 1760 Hz: −16.4 dB |

Engine controls:
- A pure sine through the engine reads aliasDb −120, roughness 9e-10, root 1.
- A raw hard-sync saw (aa 0, os 1) reads −13.5 dB against −52.2 dB clean.
- N 9 with two blades costs 11× N 1 with no blades (a ratio, never an absolute time).

### P3: `gauntlet.mjs` + `gauntlet_report.mjs`

**Sampler.** Draws are taper-space, one substream per parameter, and the tree is applied as a fixpoint: inactive parameters go to their defaults, and 0 patches were unsettled. Modes are `broad` (uniform) and `edge` (the fuzzer: extremes with probability 0.7).

**Measurement per patch:**
- TONAL: A3, 0.5 s.
- ALIAS: E5, measured against the same patch at 4× oversampling.
- POLY: 4 overlapping notes.

**Run.** Resumable, chunked, with an ETA line every 25 patches. Output goes to `local/patchspace/p3/`, which git ignores.

**Estimate first.** The pilot measured 0.38 s per patch single-threaded. Under the machine's real load (load average 11–29 from other sessions), the first attempt projected ~27 min. I stopped it and resumed at N = 3000 (2000 broad + 1000 edge) on 4 workers: 18.5 min wall, including 2 min of stopped passes.

**Results:**
- Yield (provisional thresholds): healthy 55.1% broad / 29.8% edge; coherent 17.7% / 23.1%.
- No NaN in 3000 patches.
- Top hotspots:
  - overload6: 20.2% overall, driven by two blades (colK/colB/fmType2/I2 bins).
  - alias: 24.3% overall, driven by the harmonic detune law (h.law 4, harmReach ×2.2–2.7).
  - Edge silence: gain 0.
- **Crushed-bells class found:** the overload6 rate climbs with N × sounding blades, to 91% at N 9 with two blades. Median cpuVoiceNorm is 25.4% inside the class (N ≥ 6, both blades) against 7.7% outside. The class is 7.9% of patches and 29.8% of overloads.
- Determinism: 6 of 6 re-measured patches reproduce every non-CPU field bit for bit.

## Alternatives rejected

- **A hand-listed tree.** The brief says derive it. The conditions are written from the guards, but each one is pinned to its guard text and checked by the probe.
- **A true JS static analyser.** The cost is disproportionate, and the anchors plus the probe give the same guarantee.
- **One undifferentiated "live" condition that includes side channels.** It would tell morph and the mod matrix that the Mod rate matters on a Crush blade; the role/side split keeps that a finding rather than a feature.
- **A 4× sample-rate aliasing reference.** The control rate and drift draws change with the sample rate. The oversampling reference was chosen, with its cap caveat stated.
- **A strict byte-compare on anchor line numbers.** It would redden on every lab round.
