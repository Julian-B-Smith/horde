### The cause: not feedback. The phase centre of a balanced swarm

Read with scratch probes on the port (not in the repo), each with a control that must read near zero:

- **The two sides differ on the FIRST sample.** `:: chord`, frame 0: the JS writes 6.825e-07 and the C++ differs from it by 2.3e-06 of that value (3.5e-06 at nudge 6). The absolute miss is small only because the preset's 1.5 s attack is still quiet. Per 128-frame block the miss is 5e-06 to 1.4e-04 of the block's peak from block 0 on (blocks 0 to 31). Controls: `:: repeat` differs by 3e-13 of its peak in block 0 and never reaches 1e-10; the ring's `:: chord` differs by 6e-14 of its peak in block 0 and stays under 1e-12 for its first 11 000 frames.
- **It is not the note-on's settle loop, and the phases do not part.** With the note-on frequencies one ULP up, the port's member phases after the four note-ons, before any sample, move by at most 9.4e-16 of a cycle.
- **It is `frame` on a swarm with no phase centre.** At detune 0 the members of a note share one frequency, and coupling -0.3 settles them into a balanced arrangement (six phases a sixth of a cycle apart) whose order parameter r goes to zero. The preset has `frame` 1, so each blade sits at its member's lead over psi = atan2(Ry, Rx) (`razor-core.js`, `couple`: the `lead` line, and `c = s.c + rotAll + mm.cOff + (d.frame ? mm.lead : 0)` in the render loop). That is the angle of a vector of length r, so a last-bit difference between V8's and the platform's sin and cos is divided by r. This is B461's mechanism, held permanently instead of passed through.
  - The chord's four voices start at r 6.4e-05, 2.6e-07, 2.9e-10 and 4.8e-11, and fall from there (by block 100: 1.1e-05, 1.8e-08, 8.0e-12, 4.3e-13). A rounding of 1e-16 over r 4.8e-11 is 2e-06, the size of the first sample's miss (consistent with it; the probes do not attribute the miss to one voice).
  - `:: repeat` plays one key, the first voice only: r 6.4e-05 falling to 1.1e-05 over its render. That is why it holds parity. It is the length of the script that keeps it there, not the preset.
  - `:: arp` has voices under r 1e-10 from about block 64.
- **So an onset window would not judge parity.** With a 384-frame window in place, the control that plants a relative pitch error from the first sample reads, on the window's max-abs: clean 3.456e-07; error 1e-12: 8.093e-07; 1e-11: 9.255e-07; 1e-10: 3.346e-06; 1e-09: 7.230e-07; 1e-08: 2.818e-06. It is not monotonic, and an error of 1e-12 moves it by 2.3. The reading follows psi's rounding, not the fault.

## 2026-10-12, later still: RULED, no exclusion. The three rows render with `frame` 0 (ADR-217 A2)
