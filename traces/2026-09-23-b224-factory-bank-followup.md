- **The one non-description-text change, verified and reasoned through, not
  silently accepted:** every regenerated preset JSON's `"build"` field moved
  (`"855f8d6"` -> the new commit's short hash). This field is a build/git
  provenance stamp (`tools/build_stamp.cmake`, `HYPERSAW_BUILD_ID` compile
  definition from `HS_GIT_HASH`, computed at CMake **configure** time) —
  outside the `"params"` object, read by nothing (`hypersaw_clap.cpp:8918`,
  "B100: provenance only, never read back"), and by the tool's own design
  updated on every regeneration regardless of what triggered it. It is not a
  parameter value. Getting a CLEAN stamp (no trailing `+` dirty marker) took
  three attempts: the compile-time `HS_GIT_HASH` is fixed at `cmake`
  **configure**, not at build, so simply rebuilding after committing the
  source fix was not enough — the tree also had to be committed and clean at
  the moment of a fresh `cmake -S ... -B build-release` reconfigure, THEN
  built, THEN the generator run, with no edits in between any of those
  steps. Verified with a JSON-diff script (every top-level key compared
  across all 41 files before/after, `build` excluded) and independently by
  `bank_check`'s own "re-save is bit-identical to the file" assertion — 0
  failures, including the three coupling-law exemplars (cloud-to-lock, splay
  interference, consonance gravity) whose lines this commit also touched.
