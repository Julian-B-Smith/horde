# b461-frame-hold-d6 — the blade's frame holds its course where the swarm's centre is undefined (D6 `frameHold`, OFF by default)

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B461, under ADR-216 (the human's ruling of 2026-10-12, items 1 and 3, and the lead's constants under that delegation). Built by an implementer dispatch from the lead, amended mid-task by the lead (the check's pad settings are pinned in the check; no parity row is built on the bank's Breathing pad).
- **Why:** with `frame` on, a member's blade sits at `c + lead`, and `lead` is measured from psi, the angle of the summed phase vectors, every 32 samples. Where the order parameter R passes through zero psi is undefined and swings by up to half a cycle in one tick, and every blade jumps: the click the human heard inside one held note. D6 gives each voice a frame that advances with the members' own mean phase advance, is corrected toward psi in proportion to R at a bounded rate, and hands each lead to its blade as a ramp. It is a flag in the C++ engine and in the composed JS, default OFF, so no existing digest moves (ADR-187 A1).

## What changed

- **C++** (`h2/engine/engine.h`, `h2/engine/blade.h`): the key `frameHold`; `holdFrame()`, called from `couple()`; the members' summed phase advance (`Voice::fhAdv`) taken in `stepMember()` where the phases step; the lead's ramp, one share at the tick and one after each later sample's last read (the end of `renderCallK`'s sample loop); `spread()` reads the lead under the frame (`leadT`) for spread law 3. With the flag off every expression of the old path is evaluated as before.
- **JS** (`docs/design/scalpel-horde-engine.js`): the same law, the same arithmetic in the same order: `holdFrame()`, `couple()`, `spread()`, `stepM()`, `renderPlain()`, `HORDE_D.frameHold`. One JS-only mechanism, `m.leadR`: the oracle's `couple()` overwrites `m.lead` on every call and its render reads `m.lead` by name, so the ramp lives in `leadR` and is copied into `lead`.
- **Lab** (`docs/design/scalpel-interface-lab.html`): one chip, `D6 frame`, OFF by default, `?d6=1` starts it on; five sites, each beside D1–D3's.
- **Ledger** (`docs/port/divergences.json`): entry D6, built, flag `frameHold`, default 0, ADR-216.
- **Parity** (`tools/h2_engine_render.mjs`): 20 flag-on rows, `C/D6 ...`, appended after every earlier row. Floors raised 543 → 563 in `tools/h2_engine_parity_check.cpp` (`kMinScenarios`) and `tools/h2_engine_rtsan_check.py` (`MIN_SCENARIOS`).

## The law as built, and where it differs from the brief's wording

Constants as ruled by ADR-216, unchanged: g rises linearly from R 0.05 to R 0.25; the correction is limited to 8 cycles per second (256 / sr cycles per 32-sample tick); at a note's start the frame is the measured psi and the leads are set, not ramped.

Four points where the brief left a choice or the code forced one. Each is the lead's to overrule.

1. **The frame's advance is measured, not predicted.** The brief asked for `psiF + meanRate * dt` with the mean of the member frequencies "as the engine is actually advancing their phases". No field holds that: the swarm retunes the members every 16 samples, so a couple tick spans two frequencies and the first is gone by the next tick (`m.inc` at a tick is the frequency of the half-tick before the last one); a frequency glide moves them every sample; a member waiting on onset scatter does not advance. So the engine sums what it actually adds to each phase (`fhAdv`, one add per member per sample while a frame is held) and the frame advances by that sum over N.
2. **Where the correction is whole and inside the rate, the frame is assigned psi, not summed.** The brief's intended property is that the frame then equals psi bit for bit. The literal `psiP + (psi - psiP)` does not give it: measured on the pad at C3, it differs from psi on 2816 of 4571 such ticks, by up to 2.2e-16 cycles (on a locked corner patch, 4277 of 5999). With the assignment it is equal on 4571 of 4571 and 5999 of 5999.
3. **The ramp's share is taken at the END of each sample, for the next one,** not in the per-sample voice pass. The blade oracle's render reads `m.lead` with no hook before the read, so the composed JS can only move it in `stepM`, on a member's last step of the sample. The C++ does the same thing in the same place in time (after the oversampling loop, so the B441 C1 replay holds; its comment is updated). The lead reaches its target on the tick's last sample either way.
4. **Spread law 3 reads the lead under the frame, not the ramp,** so in a locked swarm its values are today's bit for bit. `spread()`'s own values are untouched.

A note-on's `couple()` on a voice that is already sounding (a mono retrigger or legato) is not a tick: the frame and the ramps stay as they stand. With one member the lead is 0 as today. With the flag switched on mid-note the first tick after it is the present law; switched off mid-note the ramp runs to the next tick and the lead is then the oracle's.

- **Flag off is bit for bit today's.** Both self-digest builds: 543 of 543 earlier rows unchanged (`--repin` reported `0 changed, 20 added, 0 removed, 543 unchanged` for each reference).

- **The one jump left at C3 is not the frame's.** Three removals of the spread law's drift each take it away, and removing the frame does not:
  - the pad with the frame off altogether (`frame` 0): 1 at C3 (0.148 at 364609), 0 at G3 and C4, flag on or off (the lead leaves the blade's position entirely);
  - the pad with Position spread 0 (`bspread` 0): 0 / 0 / 0 with the flag on, 3 / 1 / 2 with it off;
  - the pad with `driftRate` 0.0001: 0 / 0 / 0 with the flag on; with `law` 0: 0 / 0 / 0 with the flag on, 2 / 1 / 2 with it off.
  
  It is B460's: the drifting spread law's values reach the blades unsmoothed every 32 samples. At C3 the phase moves 0.0027 cycles a sample, and a drift step of the blade's position is of that size.
- **The constants do not decide it.** Scratch builds, flag on, C3 / G3 / C4: rate 4: 0 / 0 / 0; rate 16: 0 / 0 / 0; R 0.02 to 0.25: 0 / 0 / 0; R 0.1 to 0.5: 1 / 0 / 0 (a different sample). Whether B460's step falls in the 8 seconds depends on the trajectory, so a neighbouring constant "passing" would be luck. The ruled constants are kept.
- **Locked case.** Members locked by construction with the frame on (`frame` 1, `phaseMode` 2, `detune` 0, `K` 0, `onset` 0, `driftDepth` 0, `onsetScatter` 0), one held C3, 4 s, flag on against off, largest |difference| per reference patch: quarter-sync 0, defaults 6.7e-14, harmonic-stack 3.2e-14, fold-over-sync 2.7e-14, crushed-bells 2.7e-15, breathing-pad 6.9e-15, glass-horde-pad 1.3e-14, continuous-min 0. Ceiling 1e-11 (about 150 times the worst). The two loud corner patches read 2.7e-9 and 1.08, and are not judged: each turns one ULP of its own note into more (2.8e-6 and 1.33), re-measured every run.
- **What the frame does at a crossing** (the pad at C3, 8 s, 11999 ticks): R fell to 0.0018; the frame ignored psi on 1143 ticks (R under 0.05), was at its rate limit on 967, and equalled psi on 4571. On leaving the hold psi was 0.46 to 0.49 cycles away and the frame turned to it at the limit: about 60 ms of every blade moving half a cycle, where there was a jump. The largest lead move in one tick was 0.0061 cycles (the lead measured up to 0.39 under the present law).
