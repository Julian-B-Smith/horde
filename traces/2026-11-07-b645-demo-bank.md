# b645-demo-presets — a demo bank of four-corner presets for the test plug-in

- **Queue item:** B645 (the horde lead, 2026-11-07, for the human: "making a handful of global presets with 4 morph corners and all currently exposed functionality controlled ... I want to see what kinds of sounds it comes up with at this stage. I would like a diversity of types of sound"). The human re-voices the bank later, once modulation and the FX exist.
- **Pass 2** (the lead, 2026-11-07, relaying the human's first listen: Bell Foundry's B "really crunchy in the low end"; Chrome Choir "barely changes"; "all of these sounds really do just sound derivative of the supersaw"; asked for resonant growl basses, classic homages, organic sounds and flutes, and "coordinating the evolution of more blendable parameters at each corner"). Pass 1's bank is replaced; its renders are kept beside pass 2's.
- **What changed** (`tools/engine-preview/`; no engine change, no committed fixture re-recorded):
  - `demo_bank.json`: sixteen presets. Growl basses: Iron Lung, Acid Line (an acid-style squelch), Talking Sub. Classic homages: Brass Section, String Machine, Tine Piano (a digital electric piano), Hollow Pulse Pad, Wavetable Sweep, Sync Lead. Organic: Bamboo Flute, Bowed Wood, Rainglass. Others: Overtone Singer (replaces Chrome Choir), Bell Foundry (B rebuilt), Glitch Cartridge, Neon Horde (the one supersaw, for contrast). Every preset but Neon Horde uses unison 1 to 3; 11 to 23 Morphable rows differ between corners per preset. Values in each row's unit, Step rows by option name.
  - `demo_bank.h/.cpp`: the one reader, shared by `gen_bank` and `demo_render`. A corner is a base plus rows, turned into the engine's own state text by loading, applying the rows as Param events and saving; an unknown key or option, a value out of range, a value the engine refuses or clamps, a Device row in a corner, or a corner without out.level stops the build. Pass 2 adds B.1's blade-envelope routes per preset (ENV 2 or 3 to a blade's cut, cutHz or width, the forms B.1 makes; anything else stops the build).
  - `gen_bank.cpp`, `bank.h`: the demos embedded after the factory bank as `Demo (morph): <name>`, each with its corners, settings, modes and routes; the factory entries are written exactly as before (checked: main's `gen_bank` and this one write byte-identical routes and all 83 factory entries).
  - `preview_plugin.cpp`: a demo load (page, host Preset, activation) puts its four corners into the morph and sets D.2's settings, the modes, no waypoints or locks; the position stays. Its routes play through the plug-in's existing route path, as the factory bank's do. A saved chunk needs nothing new.
  - `smoke.cpp`, `gui_shot.mm`: the demo bank's checks and controls (`preview-plant` gains `HORDE_PREVIEW_PLANT_DEMO_ONE_CORNER`, `preview-plant-map` gains `HORDE_PREVIEW_PLANT_DEMO_KEEPS_CORNERS`); the window test expects the 83 factory presets, then the demos.
  - `demo_render.cpp` (`preview-demo-render`, on demand): renders and measures every demo. Pass 2 adds: the routes, played as the plug-in plays them; the low band (share of power below 150 Hz, a ceiling of 5 % for a preset not marked bass); supersaw-ness ((members − 1) × detune × the range's factor, per sounding oscillator); the count of Morphable and Structural rows that differ between corners. It also fixes pass 1's spectral scaling (bins divided by twice the frame length, so pass 1's centroids and brightness read an octave low; its corner comparisons held, all scaled alike).
  - `CMakeLists.txt`, `README.md`.
- **Measured** (`preview-demo-render`, engine 6cd98b5, 44.1 kHz, chord 60 64 67 71 at 0.85, each corner alone and a 16 s sweep A → B → D → C → A, single notes for leads and basses; CPU the least of 3 runs per 512-sample block, 4 notes; the machine was shared, load average 8 to 20 during the final run): no silence while held, no peak above −1 dBFS in any corner or sweep, no non-finite sample, DC at most 5e-3, unison at most 9, every corner pair at least 2.1 dB apart in third-octave colour, no corner of a non-bass preset above 2.4 % of its power below 150 Hz (pass 1's Bell Foundry B: 27 %). Supersaw-ness 0 to 0.3 everywhere but Neon Horde (6.0). Worst blocks 16 % to 62 % (Brass Section 62 % with one member; 73 % with two, so it plays one). Levels: every corner at the factory median (−18.1 LUFS) but Rainglass (−20.5, its cave corner −26.5) and Bell Foundry (−18.9), trimmed so their sweeps stay under −1 dBFS, and Glitch Cartridge, capped by its peaks.
- **Engine findings for the builder and the GUI** (the page's notes, in full there): no oscillator filter, so brightness is rebuilt per preset from a full-cycle Sync blade with a `sine->saw` carrier (its `shape`), `sub.tone` and the noise filter; resonance and formants come from blades (Sync with a sine carrier, FM reset at `cutHz`); envelope-to-timbre exists only as B.1's routes; no pitch envelope and no LFO (`bladeN.rotate` is the only periodic motion); a moving `noise.vowel` is very expensive (Overtone Singer's sweep: a mean of 98 % of a block with it moving, 40 % with it held), so the bank holds it still.
- **Quantum and the switch pick:** engine 6cd98b5 still picks each source's switch on its own (round 64 not built), so Glitch Cartridge, the one Quantum demo, keeps the same sources on in every corner; the rest use Blend, where switches fade (MO-26).
- **Evidence consulted:** SPEC-ENGINE 5.2 to 5.6, Appendix D (D.2, MO-7, MO-11, MO-13, MO-26, MO-27); the engine's `horde_engine.h` and `manifest/manifest.h`; GUI 3's `device.js` (how the page adopts corners on a load); the factory bank for idioms; B642's trace for the Quantum hole; the human's listening notes on pass 1.
- **Alternatives rejected:** a separate demo array beside `kBank` (every list, index and stats array would need a second path); waypoints in the demos (they would linger into factory loads, which keep the modes and settings); levelling inside `gen_bank` (renders at build time; the measured levels are committed in the JSON); a low-end ceiling as a level against the bank's median (spectral leakage of a loud corner with nothing below 150 Hz read as low end; the share does not).
- **Checks:** see the commit message and the lead's report: `build.sh 6cd98b5` (bridge test, smoke with all three plants, window) and `./verify fast`, whose one red gate is `tolerance_registry_check` on `demo_render.cpp`'s report thresholds, a new inline-float-compare candidate that only a human ruling may register. Not pushed until that ruling.
