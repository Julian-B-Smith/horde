# b645-demo-presets — a first demo bank of four-corner presets for the test plug-in

- **Queue item:** B645 (the horde lead, 2026-11-07, for the human: "making a handful of global presets with 4 morph corners and all currently exposed functionality controlled ... I want to see what kinds of sounds it comes up with at this stage. I would like a diversity of types of sound"). A first pass before modulation and the FX; the human re-voices the bank later.
- **What changed** (`tools/engine-preview/`; no engine change, no committed fixture re-recorded):
  - `demo_bank.json`: ten presets, each four corners, D.2's settings and any key's mode: Neon Horde (supersaw), Undertow (sub bass, growl corner), Glass Pluck, Slow Tide (pad), Bell Foundry (FM bells through the blades), Rust Resonator (noise and resonator), Chrome Choir (formant lead), Stab Machine (chord stab, Quantum), Monolith (drone), Glitch Cartridge (hits, Quantum). Values in each row's unit, Step rows by option name.
  - `demo_bank.h/.cpp`: the one reader, shared by `gen_bank` and `demo_render`. A corner is a base plus rows, turned into the engine's own state text by loading, applying the rows as Param events and saving; an unknown key or option, a value out of range, a value the engine refuses or clamps, a Device row in a corner, or a corner without out.level stops the build.
  - `gen_bank.cpp`, `bank.h`: the demos embedded after the factory bank as `Demo (morph): <name>`, each with its corners, settings and modes (`EmbeddedMorph`); the factory entries are written exactly as before (checked: main's `gen_bank` and this one write byte-identical routes and all 83 factory entries).
  - `preview_plugin.cpp`: a demo load (page, host Preset, activation) puts its four corners into the morph and sets D.2's settings, the modes, no waypoints or locks; the position stays. A saved chunk needs nothing new: corner A is the preset's state, B to D are differences as for any four-corner patch.
  - `smoke.cpp`: the demo bank's checks and their controls (below); `preview-plant` gains `HORDE_PREVIEW_PLANT_DEMO_ONE_CORNER`, `preview-plant-map` gains `HORDE_PREVIEW_PLANT_DEMO_KEEPS_CORNERS`.
  - `demo_render.cpp` (`preview-demo-render`, on demand): renders and measures every demo (README).
  - `CMakeLists.txt`, `README.md`.
- **Measured** (`preview-demo-render`, engine 6cd98b5, 44.1 kHz, chord 60 64 67 71 at 0.85, each corner alone and a 16 s sweep A → B → D → C → A, single notes for the leads and basses; CPU the least of 3 runs per 512-sample block, 4 notes): no silence while held, no peak above −1 dBFS (corners and sweeps), no non-finite sample, DC at most 4e-3, unison at most 9, every corner pair at least 2 dB apart in third-octave colour. Worst blocks: Slow Tide 55 %, Chrome Choir 43 %, Stab Machine 38 %, Rust Resonator 33 %, Glass Pluck 28 %, Bell Foundry 21 %, Neon Horde 19 %, Monolith 17 %, Glitch Cartridge 12 %, Undertow 7 %. Levels: every corner at the factory median (−18.1 LUFS) except where the −1 dBFS peak caps it (the plucks and hits, 1 to 11 dB under), and Glass Pluck, Stab Machine and Glitch Cartridge trimmed as a whole so their sweeps also stay under −1 dBFS.
- **Quantum and the switch pick:** engine 6cd98b5 still picks each source's switch on its own (round 64 not built), so the two Quantum demos keep the same sources on in all four corners; the other eight use Blend, where switches fade (MO-26).
- **Evidence consulted:** SPEC-ENGINE 5.2 to 5.6, Appendix D (D.2, MO-7, MO-11, MO-13, MO-26, MO-27); the engine's `horde_engine.h` and `manifest/manifest.h`; GUI 3's `device.js` (how the page adopts corners on a load); the factory bank for idioms; B642's trace for the Quantum hole.
- **Alternatives rejected:** a separate demo array beside `kBank` (every list, index and stats array would need a second path); keeping blade-envelope routes or waypoints in the demos (routes are the modulation stand-in; a demo's waypoints would linger into factory loads, which keep the modes and settings); levelling inside `gen_bank` (renders at build time; the measured levels are committed in the JSON instead).
- **Checks:** `build.sh 6cd98b5`: bridge test GREEN, smoke GREEN with all three plants (255 ok, 0 failed: the demo loads with rows apart in every corner, the chunk round trip, a factory preset after a demo bit-identical to the offline render, both controls), window GREEN (`gui_shot.mm` now holds the list to 83 factory presets then the demos). A fresh frames dump differs from main's own build's only in the first frame's preset list (the ten demo names appended); the display file is byte-identical; no committed fixture touched. `./verify fast`: RED on one gate only, `tolerance_registry_check`: `demo_render.cpp`'s report thresholds (`>0.01 | >0.01 | >=2.0 | >0.7`: DC, DC, corners apart, CPU) are a new inline-float-compare candidate, which only a human ruling may register (`--approve` needs a human ref). Not pushed until that ruling.
