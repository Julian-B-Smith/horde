# b584-gui3-slice3 — GUI 3, third slice: the bottom modulator bank, and the Random page folded into the Morph page

- **Queue item:** B584, third slice (ADR-322 items 2 to 4; B556, ADR-291 item 2). ADR-322 item 3, verbatim: "a strip at the bottom of every page, which can be toggled, scrolling horizontally, listing the modulators that target a parameter on that page (read from the routes); each modulator shows a few critical controls; a + button adds a modulator, which is discarded if the interface closes before it is mapped to anything; each modulator has a draggable map handle that maps it to any parameter it is dropped on." Item 4: "the shell offers both (a switch between modulators and macros, and a plain show/hide)". Item 2: "The Random tab goes" and the Morph page gains a Randomize panel. The lead's brief added the seam tests with plants and the README's reasons.
- **Why:** the human designed the bank in their own words and folded the Random page into the Morph page (ADR-322). This slice builds both in the shell. The earlier slices' traces are `2026-10-29-b584-gui3-shell.md` and `2026-10-29-b584-gui3-slice2.md`.
- **What changed:**
  - **`docs/design/gui3/device.js`:**
    - Pending modulators: `activate`, `pending` and `close`. Pending ones are outside the patch and the history, and `close` drops the unmapped ones.
    - `drop(src, key)` goes through `addRoute`. A refused drop leaves the source pending; an accepted one maps it.
  - **`docs/design/gui3.html`:**
    - The bank, at the foot of every page. One card per source, built once from the bundle's `ModCardHeader`, with a Map handle and the kind's generated critical controls, mounted through `mountControl`. The listing is read from the routes against the page's keys, plus the pending sources.
    - **+ Modulator** opens a menu of the free sources.
    - Pointer drag-to-map uses `elementFromPoint`.
    - `pagehide` calls `dev.close()`, and a "Close the interface" button simulates it.
    - Both toggles: **Mods | Macros** and **Bank**.
    - A tenth self-check covers the bank.
    - A `#page,morph,bank` review link.
    - The Random tab is removed (via `layout.json`). The Morph page has a Randomize placeholder card that lists its contents to come and links to `random-lab.html`.
  - **`docs/design/gui3/layout.json`:**
    - The Random page is removed. It placed no parameters, so the reach check stays green with 317 of 317.
    - `bank.kinds` chooses ENV 1 to 4's attack, decay, sustain and release, with the reason.
  - **`tools/gen_gui3_controls.py`:** `build_bank` generates the bank's controls from the table. It refuses a suffix a block lacks (`unknown-key`) and a choice with no reason (`bank`). Two new plants make 14.
  - **`tools/labharness/gui3_seam_check.mjs`:**
    - `pending`: + makes a source pending, outside the patch and the history; close drops it; a mapped one survives.
    - `mapdrop`: drops on stepped and reserved rows are refused and leave the source pending; a continuous one maps.
    - Three plants. The check now runs 17 tests and 16 plants.
  - **`verify`:** comments updated to the counts. Nothing removed or relaxed.
  - **`docs/design/gui3/README.md`:** the bank section with the reasons, proposals 12 to 15, gaps. `shell-data.js` and `docs/design/index.html` regenerated.

- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - **Rebuilding bank cards when the list changes:** every knob would add a view to the Session for good. Cards are built once and shown or hidden.
  - **A hand-kept list of bank modulators:** ruled out by B556; the list is read from the routes.
  - **Inventing LFO rows for "rate, shape, depth":** no LFO rows exist in SPEC-ENGINE 5 (RC-4 defers the LFOs). A kind entry adds them when they do.
  - **Hiding the Macros view:** ADR-322 item 4 asks for both toggles. With no macros it says why.
