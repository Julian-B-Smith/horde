# b545-chain-line-and-blend-maps — two new Design System components from the labs the human liked: Chain line and Blend maps

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP B545 (the human's two requests, 2026-10-22: "I liked the horizontal routing/gain staging visual from the mixer lab" and "the detailed blend maps from the morph editor lab (with the TPS and TIN delta options, the waypoints, etc.)"). Written by the implementer for the lead, who lands it in `traces/` (the brief put every file of the horde repo out of scope, so this file was left in the scratchpad).
- **Why:** (1) `ChainLine` is the mixer lab's "every tap on one line" as a glass: x the chain in signal order, y each tap's peak hold, the 0 dBFS rail dashed in `alarm`, source lanes folding into the next stage, solid / hollow / red dots, the first-over ring; it takes Gain staging's own stage list, `read` and `latch`, so it reads the same stages and sits beside it (the preview mounts both on one engine). The FX device's line starts at the audio input. (2) `BlendMaps` is the Waypoints pane: the four laws' math transcribed from the lab, a pad with pins and corner chips, one map per law with contours, TIN's dashed triangulation and the two clamp hatches, the clamp switch, the difference view and a cross-section under every law. B272's ruling is the API: `laws: ['tps', 'tind']` is the setting (TPS·Δ with clamp the default), and the lab's TIN and IDW·Δ exist only as `compare`, labelled and never selectable (the preview passes them).
- **Evidence consulted:** `docs/design/mixer-lab.html` (cluster :360, STAGES :461, `drawStaging` :991, `originStage` :1130); `docs/design/morph-editor-lab.html` (Waypoints pane :623-642, block W :1496-1700, `paintField` :2847, `drawTriangulation` :2877, `drawPins` :2891, pin write path :3044-3100, section :3136-3470, gestures :3924-4090); the project's GainStaging, Meter, CrossSection, QuantumPad, MorphGrid and Kuramoto READMEs and previews; `bundle.js` `Viz.kit`, `Synth.rng`; `50-ring-gain.js` (the stage list, the peak-hold constants); the B544 trace and its harness (copied to `b545-impl/`).
- **Alternatives rejected:** editing GainStaging to expose its peak holds (the part is out of scope), so the line repeats the hold law (1.5 s, 20 dB/s, per channel) and the README says to change them together; a pin dragged by the house relative rule (the lab's `pinMove` writes the pointer position, so the lab is followed, and the README says so); plotting an owed tap at a modelled level (Gain staging's contract is "never a guess"), so an owed tap has no reading and is hollow on the floor line; selectable comparison laws (B272 rules two options only); the lab's B232 rule, puck, stations and reach/Nth-pin measurements (proposed-not-built, or other components', or the lab's audit); a second sample engine in the bundle (the preview inlines Gain staging's, as its own preview does).
- **What changed, by file** (all under the scratchpad project): `hds-src/js/60-chain-blend.js` and `hds-src/css/60-chain-blend.css` (new parts, each an IIFE extending `window.Horde`; no other part touched, checked with `cmp` against `hds-src-pre-b545`); `components/bundle.js` and `bundle.css` rebuilt by `hds-tools/build.py` (equal to the concatenation of the parts, checked); `components/ChainLine/{README.md,preview.html}`, `components/BlendMaps/{README.md,preview.html}`; `components/index.d.ts` (the new types); `README.md` (the Components paragraph). New in the bundle: `Horde.ChainLine`, `Horde.Viz.chainLine`, `Horde.BlendMaps`, `Horde.BlendMaps.math`.
- **Verify** (no repo oracle for the scratch project; scripts and logs in `b545-impl/`): `node --check` on the rebuilt `bundle.js` and each preview's inline script, exit 0; no `Math.random|Date.now|performance.now|new Date` in the new files; build.py output equals the concatenation of the parts; both previews at 320, 360, 480 and 960 px in light and dark: no element past the right edge and no console errors (16 runs, `fit.log`); the harness's text-contrast audit "text ok" in both themes. Behaviour (`behave.log`): the chain line lists 20 level taps for the instrument and starts at `in` for the FX device; its first over is the make-up (as Gain staging's) and moves to FX 1 IN when that lamp is cleared; the MUST-READ-ZERO control (make-up near 0 dB, lamps cleared, 6 s) latches nothing in either view; with "Sources + output fed" the other taps are hollow on the floor line; the blend maps' pin drag moves a pin and every canvas redraws, the law switches (pills and minis), the clamp switch moves the range-clip share from 0 to 11.5 % of the pad with it off, the difference view redraws, double-click adds and right-click deletes a pin, a drag onto corner A is refused and the pin stops at its last legal spot, the keyboard moves a pin and its value, and the section end drags. The law properties of the lab's own self-check hold over 60 seeded fixtures (mulberry32 235) x 4 laws: no pin is today's bilinear bit for bit (P1), corners bit-identical (P2), every law passes through every pin (P3), TIN never leaves its hull (P4), a first pin at the heard value changes nothing under TIN·Δ, IDW·Δ and TPS·Δ (P5), with the control (plain TIN is not today's blend) reading non-zero on 55 of 60. No git hash: the scratchpad project is not a repo.
- **Open questions:** (1) A pin's mark is the lab's diamond and corner A's glyph is also a diamond; they are told apart by ink and the corner chips: the human may want a different pin shape. (2) The limiter's gain reduction is not on the line (a reduction, not a level); a bracket from LIM IN to the output is possible if wanted. (3) The y range is the lab's -48 to +6 while Gain staging's bars run -66 to +6: whichever the mixer panel's ruling picks (B402). (4) Which taps are owed is the consumer's to report; the lab's FED / PART / OWED table was written against an older shell. (5) `design-system.json` and the `@ds-bundle` component list at the top of `bundle.js` (00-core) name neither new component (nor GainStaging, which B544 left out of that list too); both are out of scope here, so the publisher should add them. (6) The pad's section line ends at the pad edge are half clipped by the glass, as in the lab. (7) The pin hit radius is 14 px, not the lab's 11, for the 28 px house target: a deliberate divergence, stated in the README.
- **Landed by the lead, 2026-10-22:** the three mountable components (GainStaging, ChainLine, BlendMaps) added to the bundle's component list in `00-core`, rebuilt, and published to the horde Design System artifact as version 21.
