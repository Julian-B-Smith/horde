B223 · the Coupling × Detune pad has to show modulation — three treatments of the same live state

The human: "The K x detune XY grids need some way to visualize modulation." Today the pad shows only where the puck IS (gui2.html:1682). Below: one pad, drawn three ways, all reading the same store; the pill picks which one every pad in the frames above uses. Magenta ring = your authored value, which never moves on its own. Violet = what the engine does to it (ADR-137's colour law). SWARM RIDE moves Swarm 1: LFO 1 on Detune, LFO 2 and Motion (M3) on Coupling. Load GLASS CHOIR to see morph move the ring itself (ringed in the owning corner's hue), and INIT for the must-read-zero case: no routes, nothing violet.
  • REACH BOX (recommended) — ADR-137's reach arc in two dimensions: the rectangle is every value the live routes can reach, the dot is where the value is now. It reads in a still screenshot and when a slow LFO is paused. One axis modulated gives a band. This is the same grammar as the knobs, so a player learns it once.
  • GHOST + TRAIL — the dot plus where it has been. Two LFOs at unrelated rates draw a Lissajous figure, which shows the shape of the motion better than the box does. The lab computes its last six seconds from the LFOs' own law. The shell could not always do that: an envelope, a sample & hold or a MIDI source has no past it can recompute, so there the trail would be a remembered history. It is also noisy on the MAIN page's small pad.
  • EDGE RAILS — the interior stays clean for the puck; each axis gets the knob's arc and live tick along its edge. This is the least clutter, but on a 150 px pad the rails are thin and the joint position (which the pad is for) is left for you to infer.
  • Source colour — not drawn. Sources are named by glyph (L1, M3) at the axis they move. A per-source hue needs a palette, which is an ADR-116 amendment that the mod-matrix lab (B207) left open. The box could take the source colour once one exists.
  • What the pad means for decision 5: a pad marks both of its addresses as visible, so the strip at the bottom of each frame stops listing Detune and Coupling while a pad shows them. The pad has to carry their motion, which is why B223 is needed.

What is exposed today, and what each face reaches

Counted at load from the embedded table: a control is designed when its row names a chunk, is not shown_when = never and is not widget = none — the same three conditions that decide whether gui2 renders it. SPECTRA's rows carry no chunk (the engine is parked), which is most of the OSC gap. A face "reaches" an address by showing a control for it (both oscillators count, since the face's OSC pill switches between them); a macro reaches its routed targets indirectly, and that is counted separately because it changes with the preset.
