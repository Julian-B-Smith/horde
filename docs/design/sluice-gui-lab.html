Use it

Click and drag on the glass. Tab walks the controls, ↑↓ rows, ←→ turn or step, Enter presses (on the XY it grabs, then arrows move it), Esc goes back, 1–4 are the soft keys. Double-click a knob to centre it. The four knobs under the glass are the patch's four macros.

Soft key 1 opens the menu. A control that can't act right now isn't drawn, so things appear and go as you switch modes (GUI-21).

What is mock on this page

Nothing here is Sluice's sound or Sluice's engine. Real, read at load: the controls, their ranges and tapers, the macro targets, the divisions, the patches and presets.
