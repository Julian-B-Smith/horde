Shriek · automaton wordmark

Colour candidates

Every plate runs your Automaton Wordmark.html: the same five phases (emerge, condense, hold, corrupt, flood) and the same rules (B3/S23, then B2/S), with the plate inverting at each flood. Only the colours change. The multi-colour plates shade each live cell by its age (newborn, young, settled), and the held word takes the settled colour. The palettes are horde's own: Shriek's module screen, the lab's screen schemes and the chassis.

On Shriek's own screen

A real frame of the screens workshop's 80s WORKSTATION skin for Shriek (fx-screens-workshop.html, 320 × 288, 1-bit), captured from a local preview; the workshop still titles the window MAW. The skin is two colours by rule (paper and ink, greys as ordered dither), so the frame is shown in each scheme exactly. The automaton runs live in a 104 × 24 title box at (9, 30), widened from the workshop's 96 × 24 slot so the word clears it by 4 cells each side, one cell per screen pixel; it is 1-bit itself, so it needs no conversion.

The word at 96 × 24

The word is only the automaton's mask: cells condense onto it, hold it, and decay from it. So the letterforms are decided by how that mask is cut at 96 × 24. Each crop below is the same real frame, live. The buttons above choose which word the two big screens run.
