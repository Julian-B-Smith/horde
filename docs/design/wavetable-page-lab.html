Q3 · The viewer: six views, switched on the glass, and an XY you drag (GUI-66; which views stay is open)

Switch views with the pips (or the name, set by Switch above) at the glass's top left, or with the cards below. Drag the glass: up and down scrubs the frame, left and right bends it, and Shift with left and right sweeps the sync ratio up from ×1, which is no sync: sync is always on now, so a morph between ratios is continuous. Hold Option (Alt) to move a quarter as far. Each drag is one undo step. Double-click the glass to put Bend back to 0. Under it, the output scope shows the oscillator's cycle after everything: the frame, spread, sync, window, softness, asym, bend and PM, over the raw frame as a dotted trace (GUI-67). With the glass focused, the arrow keys do the same. The pill at the bottom right shows what each direction moves; click it to map any of the nine wavetable controls to each, or to invert one. The view and the mapping are one choice for every viewer, remembered on this machine. With morph on, the corner tables sit in a plain row under the viewer; where they go is Q1's question. Every shape here is a MOCK.

Q2 · Ruled (GUI-65, GUI-67): the PM matrix as cells, on a card on the Osc dashboard and a row on each Osc page

Your pick: P1 whole (the dashboard card and the Osc-page row) and the signal flow. The global signal flow touches too much for this lab and gets its own (GB38). Then: "Cells are probably the way to go after all, in both cases", so the card is C1 and the row R1 (GUI-67). The other options stay below as the record. Rows are the oscillator being modulated, columns the modulator, and the diagonal is each oscillator's own feedback (WT-61). Every depth is a corner value like any other control. Drag any depth up or down; the card and the row share the same values.
