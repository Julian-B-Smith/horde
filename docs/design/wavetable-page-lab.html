Wavetable page lab

The wavetable member replaces the blades in 1.0, and there are three oscillators (ADR-387). This lab asks where two of the new things go, each shown inside a horde window at its real size. The Osc page's lead picture is already yours: the blended frame stack the oscillator reads at the puck, with its cycle and harmonics.

Drag the small pad's puck, or the puck in any variant that has one, and every picture follows: the tables blend by the corner weights, Linear. Every table is a MOCK drawn from eight seeded generators named after the factory set's. No audio. The grey boxes on the left are the swarm's pictures, unchanged by these questions.

Q2 · Where the 3 × 3 PM matrix lives

Rows are the modulated oscillator, columns the modulator; the diagonal is each oscillator's own feedback (WT-61). Every cell is a corner value like every other control, so it blends across the pad. Drag a cell, or a wire, up or down to set its depth.
