Is there a reason to keep two mixers?

No signal reason. The console's OSC 1, OSC 2, SUB and NOISE are Gain staging's first four stages: the same tap and the same gain. Today they're also out of step, because only the console's faders are wired: the self-check measures this below.

One real difference, which one console can show. The five faders with engine rows morph and take modulation. The other sixteen gains are global and never morph (B435), and only the sources have on, mute and solo. So an arrow in a corner colour moves with the corners, and a plain ink arrow doesn't.

What it costs. A source strip goes from 104 px to 66 px, and the other stages to about 33 px, so their readouts lose “dB”. There's no reason to keep them separate unless you want the sources to stay large, or the sixteen other stages out of sight most of the time.

A third control is still here. The chain line above the console has its own gain bars. In C1 and C2 they move the same gains, and the chain line takes a lot of the page, so you can judge whether you still want it.

Things to know before you choose

One ruler in C1 and C2. You chose one scale, with each meter on its own fader's scale. That can't stretch to the whole chain: on a ±24 dB fader, a meter would show nothing under −24 dBFS. So these two forms put every strip on one ruler, −66 to +26 dB. 0 dB is one straight line across the console, and each arrow travels only its own range. The cost is the tall red zone, the top 28%, because out.level reaches +26 dB.

The scale. Today the fader and the meter have different scales, and it doesn't show because they're side by side. Out is the clear case: its 0 dB is at 70% of the fader but 92% of the meter. Put one on the other and that would be a lie, so M1 to M4 draw each meter on its own fader's scale. That means Out's red over zone is its top 30%, because its fader goes up to +26 dB. (That's M1 to M4. C1 and C2 use the one ruler above.)

Where the room comes from. In a vertical strip the merge alone saves nothing. M0 to M3 all measure 574 × 285 px at W1 and 414 × 285 px at W2, because a strip’s width is set by its name, its peak and lamp, and M and S, not by the fader. Today’s strip fits W2 too. Only M4 changes the console’s shape. ADR-333 has you asking for a little more room here, so W1 keeps it.

What a cap covers. M1's cap sits on the bars and hides what's under it. M2 and M3 hide nothing, but their handle is smaller to grab.
