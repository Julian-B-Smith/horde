The foot row: six options

The fixed info box (ADR-335 r4) keeps GUI 3's foot row at 216 px, though the bank is only 68 px (ADR-338 r7), so ten of the fourteen pages scroll and a band above the bank sits empty. Six options, each applied to the real shell in a frame and measured there. Your pick becomes the ruling (B589).

Under every option: text past the box's edge can't be read

The box's text scrolls, but the pointer can't reach it: moving onto the box clears the box back to its idle line, so there is nothing left to scroll. Today that hides 2 descriptions; F1 would hide about 76. The fix is small and in the shell (keep what is showing while the pointer is on the box), and it isn't drawn here. F1 and F2 need it. It's worth doing whichever you pick.
