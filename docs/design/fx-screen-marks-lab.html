FX screen marks lab

For every FX screen: different ways to show a control's morph corners and its modulation without corner colours, each with its cost, and a choice per screen for the human. All of it is sample data.

How to use it. Drag the puck in the bar, or press Walk, and start or stop the modulator: every glass follows. Below each screen, click one corner card and one modulation card; the big glass on the left puts your two together. The last section lists what you chose. Stop the modulator to judge a still frame.

Everything on a glass is sample data. Four rows: TONE (continuous, four different corner values, a live route from LFO 1), DRIFT (under Quantum, it lands on one owning corner), MODE (stepped) and MIX (no route, the control: any mod mark on it would be a fault). The morph law is a stand-in, and the real owner is read from the engine. Corners go above each track and modulation below, as the Map Mode Spec has it.
