Finding your way

  • Menus on the glass: * FILE PAGE INPUT AUDIO VIEW. Soft keys 1 and 2 step the pages.
  • Soft key 3 swaps what the four knobs turn: this preset's macros, or the fixed row.
  • Soft key 4 is the page's own action, when it has one.
  • Click or drag anything on the glass. Double-click returns a slider to its default, or a macro to its home.
  • Keyboard, with the glass focused: Tab, arrows (Shift is fine), Enter, Esc, 1 to 4, Page Up and Down.
  • Editing a pad value jumps the point to the corner it lands on, so you hear what you save. Stepped keys belong to the whole patch.

Readings to rule on

  • Knobs: four knobs, up to eight controls. I gave soft key 3 the swap between macros and fixed row.
  • Scheme: opens on the rack's amber on near-black (ADR-273), and the seven schemes are selectable, with paper as ground and ink as physics (the skin's rule).
  • GUI-21 at zero: Tone Pivot, Compressor Low Cut and the loop's rows leave when their amount is exactly 0. A Mix of 0 doesn't blank the screen, and Drive stays on the fixed row with every stage off.
  • Curve B stays drawn at morph 0, because you set it before morphing to it.
  • Snapshots: a save adds a fresh snapshot and binds that corner to it, and never replaces one. Past four, the ones that aren't factory can be deleted (GUI-24). There's no cap yet: the save budget that sets one isn't ruled (B603), and a patch with more than four is more than horde's corner state holds today (ADR-300).
