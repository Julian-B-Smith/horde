A listening prototype for desynchronising the blade from the carrier. SCALPEL's window lives in the carrier's phase, so the output repeats at f₀. Here the window runs on its own clock: Hz (the oracle's Rotate, driven past its ±4 Hz range, no new law) or × f₀ (the window advances r·f₀, so the cut recurs at (1 − r)·f₀ on every key; the one divergence). An A/B switches between the oracle, bit-identical, and the clock. A harmonic-grid spectrum shows the partials leave the grid. Aliasing, DC and edge clicks are measured against a 4×-rate render, and 12 demo presets sit beside 8 of the bench's.

Open questions — for the human, after listening

  1. Keep it at all? This page cannot answer that. Nothing here says the clock sounds good or bad; the A/B and the demos exist so the ear can.
  2. Hz, × f₀, or both. Hz is already the oracle's law (only its ±4 Hz range is a bench UI limit), and its relation to the note changes on every key. × f₀ keeps the relation on every key, and it is the one new law (a divergence, an ADR).
  3. The ratio's range. Built as −2…2. At r = 1 the window rides the carrier and the cut stops recurring (the blade freezes into a gain and an offset). Near r = 1 the edges chatter (see the edge table). Should the range stop short of 1, or should a port fix the edge instead?
  4. The edge fix a port would need. The window moves once per sample and the carrier once per substep, so a moving edge can jump over the carrier without being band-limited. A C++ port would detect edge crossings in window-relative phase. That is a change to the band-limiting in stepM, not to the rotation, so it is out of this lab's scope.
  5. The DC estimate. The oracle's per-cycle estimate (DC mode 2) assumes a still window. The DC table shows where it helps and where it adds sub-audio wobble under the clock; the blocker (mode 1) is the oracle's other option.
  6. Blade 2. Built as either following blade 1's clock or on its own (Hz or × f₀). Is a second clock worth a control?
  7. Morph and modulation (B275's readiness fields). Not built. The ratio would need a morph behaviour, and its maximum viable modulation rate and pitch relation are still open.
