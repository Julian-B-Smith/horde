Which edge correction should the swarm and the SCALPEL blade use? The audit (B378 F3) measured the core's 2-point polyBLEP folding from C3 up (−41.6 dB at C4, −26.8 at C8) and a 16-tap-a-side BLEP table at ≤ −120 dB. This lab plays six corrections — polyBLEP, polyBLEP at 2×, a linear-phase table, a minimum-phase table, and two note-dependent schemes — on a unison saw, a single saw and a sync blade, level-matched and blind, and measures each live with B346's estimator, a worker-timed CPU readout, its latency and what it keeps in 16–20 kHz.
TEST HARNESS, NOT THE ENGINE: the oscillators here are clean standalone saws and a sync saw written only to carry edges into the correction under test. Nothing on this page is swarm_core.h, an h2 core or the SCALPEL oracle, and nothing here changes them. The numbers compare corrections; they are not the engine's numbers.

§4 16–20 kHz — what each option keeps, and what lands there

ONE SAW AT
One saw, steady state, 1 s, spectrum by metrics.mjs (Blackman-Harris, 8192). kept: the power in the harmonic lobes between 16 and 20 kHz against an additive band-limited saw's (every harmonic below Nyquist, no roll-off). landed: the power in the same band OUTSIDE the harmonic lobes (aliases), against the same ideal. The ideal is drawn as the pale trace.
