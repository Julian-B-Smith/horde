Combharmonic coherence of the sum

Per harmonic h, |Σᵢ e^{ihθᵢ}|/N: how much of that harmonic survives the sum. Locked = every tooth full. Splayed = only multiples of N survive (amber), the rest cancel — a saw at N× the pitch. The cloud is a ragged comb in between.

Chainspins in a row

One arrow per voice, in voice order, at its phase (relative to voice 0). Coupled = all parallel. Anti-coupled = a uniform twist of 2π/N per step, a helix seen edge-on. Random = a jumble. Parallel and twisted don't look alike the way bunched and spread do.

Signsigned coherence

s = r − rsplay. One needle, centered at zero: +1 is lock, −1 is splay, and the random cloud rests near the middle because both order parameters are small there. This is the meter that reads K's sign back to you.

Ringphase circle

The textbook view. Each voice is a spoke at its phase θᵢ; the ivory arrow is the mean field r·e^{iψ}. Locking is the spokes bunching.

Wellmean-field potential

Every voice feels V(θ) = −K·r·cos(θ−ψ). K deepens the bowl; below zero the same bowl is drawn in the splay frame φᵢ=θᵢ−2πi/N; each ball settles where the slope equals its own detune, so the splay is the natural-frequency spread made physical.

Weavephase raster

Time runs left→right, one row per voice, brightness = cos θᵢ. Incoherent voices make moiré; locked voices make one straight weave. This is what a supersaw is, unrolled.

Splaynatural vs. effective frequency

x = the detune you dialed in, y = the frequency each voice actually runs at. Uncoupled sits on the diagonal; fully locked collapses to a flat line. K flattens the cloud.

Sumthe waveform

Audio-rate saws with the swarm's current phase offsets, summed. Faint lines are the individual voices. Interference is timbre.

Tugpairwise coupling matrix

Cell (i,j) is the force voice j exerts on voice i: sin(θⱼ−θᵢ−α), scaled by |K|. Amber pulls forward, blue pulls back. Below zero it's the same force measured in the splay frame. Antisymmetric when α = 0; α breaks that and the swarm rotates.

Coherencer over time

The order parameter, 0 = cloud, 1 = lock. Amber is r, blue is rsplay (coherence of the equidistant frame). Dashed line is the mean-field prediction √(1−Kc/|K|). Kick the phases and watch it recover.

Orbitmean field in the complex plane

The path traced by the tip of r·e^{iψ}. A cloud makes a wandering knot near the origin; a locked swarm makes a clean circle near the rim.
