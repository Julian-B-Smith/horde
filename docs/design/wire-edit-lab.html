Wires you can grab

This is the shell's real Mod page with wires you can select, drag and delete. Each question has options, and changing one applies straight away. The first option in each is what I'd pick. Ordinary wires are real routes; depth wires are MOCK until the engine has them (B70).

Try: click a wire. Drag its ◇ up and down. Drag a ring at its end to another box. Grab the dotted wire. Right-click a wire. Press Delete.

In every option

  • A click on a wire selects it, and its parameter becomes the selected one. A click on nothing, or Esc, lets go.
  • Shift drags finely. A double-click on a ◇ puts it back to 25%. A Control's ◇ widens and thins its band, as its chip does.
  • Delete or Backspace removes the selected wire, and its depth wires go with it. Right-click has polarity, reset and remove.
  • One level only: a depth wire can be moved, but nothing can land on it, and a wire with depth wires on it can't become one (B70's restriction).
  • The same source can't have two wires to one destination, as in the grid. A second Control on one parameter is refused (ADR-266).
