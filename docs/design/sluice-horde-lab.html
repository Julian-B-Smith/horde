Sluice as horde will host it, rebuilt to Sluice's own architecture spec. The Play face (the default), the Rack face (the machinery) and the Scope, each built at load from Sluice's spec and manifest, read in place through a gitignored local link. Nothing of Sluice's is committed here. The sound is horde's v0.1 NETWORK reference, not Sluice's netcore (see B328); where the spec has no v0.1 counterpart the control is drawn and marked "not voiced in v0.1". Round 2 (B337 + B338) is a horde proposal, pending Sluice (brief seq 10): a snapshot library per patch with a binding per corner and unsaved drafts; four fixed macros plus four variable ones; corner chips filled only when selected; and a Game Boy Color skin of the Play face. Round 3 (B348) made the spec's Play face (by its own name) the default, with a way into the full device, and made spill-over against hard bypass visible; its per-corner patches on Sluice's own XY are withdrawn by ADR-188. Round 4 (B353, ADR-188 + A1): Sluice's XY holds ONE patch again. Different Sluice patches live at the corners of horde's morph XY (the MAIN tab, a MODEL of horde's quantum-morph field), each with its own Sluice-XY position, a parameter horde can map to its macros; parity blends, otherwise the live patch flips with a 0.1 buffer and its dry/wet glides. Macros are the patch's; each snapshot sets its own range. Tails are in SETTINGS. Round 5 (B361): one shape system (every knob cap and corner chip round); the GBC skin is the whole instrument, every function on its screen, by pointer and keyboard; and the FX page follows horde's morph into the live corner.
