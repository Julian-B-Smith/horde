What the two synths do today

horde's engine. Every source follows ENV 1 unless its Envelope row picks ENV 2, 3 or 4 (RC-6.5). There's no option for a source to follow nothing.

Serum 2. It has four envelopes, Env 1 always sets the output level, and a source or filter routed to the main output can skip Env 1 with an Env button on the MIX page. Anything else about an envelope is a modulation route. So horde can already do what Serum 2's button is for, and more, with no route to make. What's left is making that choice easy to see.
