Campaign-2 item 6 — the three phase-domain axes from the 2026-07-25 timbre research, prototyped together because they share one aliasing budget. Every axis keeps the waveform a rising ramp per cycle, so the saw mandate holds: sync resets the ramp early, warp changes its rate, ripple perturbs it. Nothing here replaces the sawtooth with another waveform. Audition lab, spec-in-code — NOT a reference. Live-style keys: ASDFGHJKL whites · WE · TYU · OP blacks · Z/X octave · space drone.

Axis 1 — hard sync

−136 dB vs −62 dB alias
Ratio 1 = inert (plain saw). ← coupling is the differentiated control: a₁ tracks the swarm's order parameter R, so the stack gets harder as it locks and softens as it splays — sync driven by the physics, not by an LFO. Spread gives each voice its own ratio.

Axis 2 — phase warp (formant)

d = v is a plain saw. Moving them apart makes the ramp run fast then slow (or the reverse) — still a monotone rising ramp, so still a sawtooth, but the slope break puts a formant peak in the spectrum. Not a filter: position and bandwidth are set by the same two numbers, so they cannot be dialled independently — which is why this is drawn as a phase map, not a resonance knob.

Axis 3 — ripple

A bounded sinusoidal perturbation of the phase — the continuum of subtle sawtooth variants that retargets ADR-058's saw↔square morph. index chooses the character: shaped keeps the map monotone (still a saw); fold restores the pre-refactor behaviour — the ripple reads the previous stage's phase, so with warp engaged the map folds backward and you get the notchy pulse wave. Fold needs warp to do anything.

Axis 4 — windowed carrier (formant)

Top trace = the grain window at the pitch you play; middle = the carrier running inside it; bottom = their product, one master cycle. The readout gives the carrier's actual Hz — if it turns red, the formant is below the fundamental and cannot be expressed, so the lock has clamped and the effect is gone. That silent clamp was the bug you were hearing.
The VOSIM/FOF construction: the window repeats at the pitch you play (its width sets formant bandwidth), while the carrier — the sync'd ramp — sets formant centre. With formant lock the carrier is tuned to an absolute Hz, so the formant STAYS PUT while pitch moves under it. Carrier purity blends the carrier toward sine: measured necessity, since a saw carrier spreads energy over its own harmonics and smears the formant. Amplitude-domain — this axis leaves saw territory by design.

Saw ↔ square (capped)

ADR-058's morph, re-admitted and hard-capped at 0.5 — enough saw integrity to still count as a saw, while reaching the hollow character. Both saws carry polyBLEP, so the morph stays anti-aliased.
