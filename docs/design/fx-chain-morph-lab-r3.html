fx-chain morph · round 3

B265 ROUND 3 LAB STATIC NODES · CABLES EMERGE AND RETREAT · ONE MODULE PER TYPE · ADR-193 · ADR-195
Round 3 takes the human's redirection literally: every module sits static and warm at a fixed place (Drive, Filter, ECHO, Scape, Shriek, Bulwark, EQ and Sluice stand-ins, one of each), and the morph moves only the cables between them. Round 2's logic still decides which cables should exist and in what order at each pad point; the cables glide there. Reorders make transient loops, raced two ways: (a) a loop-gain bound with one block of delay on the backward cable, or (b) ordered fades over a bypass. Silent nodes sleep with their state kept. A click metric measures every transition against round 2's insert/remove behaviour, which an A/B toggle plays. It sounds, it measures itself, and a self-check plants one fault per claim.
