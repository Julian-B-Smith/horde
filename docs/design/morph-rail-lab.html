Drawing a rail, and per-parameter distribution

The two parts of the Morph page with no design yet. A rail limits the morph to a path you draw, with one control moving along it. Try each way of drawing below. There's no sound: the strip under the pad shows what the path passes through.

The rail

On another page's small pad the rail and puck are drawn, and it can't be edited there (B133).

What the path passes through

Each line is a real row, unrolled along the rail from its start to its end, in the colour of the corner that owns it there. A stand-in, not MO-7's law.

Per-parameter distribution

B28 asked to edit how much of the pad each corner owns, row by row. It was written before a row could own its regions (GN-6), so these options ask how much of it is left to add. Select a row to see its regions.

For you to rule

  1. How you draw a rail: Bézier handles, points it passes through, or drawn by hand. My pick is points, with hand drawing as a shortcut that gives you points. Handles offer more control than most people will want on a 320 px pad.
  2. Whether the puck scrubs along the rail while it's on, as it does here. The alternative is that the pad is entirely read-only and only the position control moves it.
  3. What distribution means: D1 adds nothing, and D2 adds bars that write the same state. D3 adds a number to every row. My pick is D2, because it needs no new engine state beyond GN-6. It also closes B28.
  4. Is the rail in 1.0? It needs two new host rows, a saved path and a spec round, and B133 says its value is mostly in being driven, so it comes after the modulators. If it's later, the page simply doesn't show it.
