Fidelity programme — seven checks × every type, each with a must-fail control
Stated as checks, not taste. Every cell is a measurement on the type's own running DSP (48 kHz, fresh instance, seeded input) against a tolerance written in the column head, and every column carries a control — a planted fault or a known-bad signal that the check MUST fail (its reading is the small second line). A control that passes would mean the check is blind; it is drawn with a red outline and counts as an error. A FAIL is a finding about the type, reported as it is — never met by loosening a tolerance. The same code runs headless in tools/labharness/filter_fidelity_check.mjs (wired into ./verify fast), which also pins today's verdicts. B290 re-cut four checks (ruled 2026-09-26): C3's level is relative to the unfiltered source; C4 gates at the plugin's real mod tick (≈ 172 Hz) and C4b reports the fastest modulation each type survives; C5 judges the nominal drive 0.3 and COST is reported beside it; C6 measures two geometries, fixed and keytracked. The last two columns are REPORTS, never gated. Round 3 changed no type, so no verdict moved.

ADAA first — every anti-aliasing option for LADDER and MS-20, through the same checks
The human asked whether antiderivative anti-aliasing could reduce the need for oversampling, so each option runs through C5 (at the nominal drive; full drive is reported beside it) and through the checks ADAA's half-sample delay threatens: C1 and C2 (the small-signal filter) and C3 (self-oscillation's pitch), plus C7 for DC. ADAA ON THE WHOLE tanh breaks the zero-delay solve (C1). ADAA ON THE RESIDUAL tanh − identity keeps the linear filter exact, but its delay detunes self-oscillation (C3), and the CENTRED window that removes the delay gives back most of the suppression. The chosen option is the cheapest that passes every check. Measured live, one option per frame, after the table above.

The types — and why each is on the menu
One interface for all of them (TYPES in the DSP section): make() an instance with set(sr, P) at control rate and tick(x) per sample, H(f) its analytic small-signal response (what the curve draws and C1 checks), and claim(P) — where its cutoff and resonance knobs say the landmark is (what C2 checks). P is the same six knobs for every type: cutoff, modulation (octaves), resonance, drive, morph, mode; a type ghosts the knobs it does not read.
Proposed changes to the swarm bank's law — beside reference/swarmfilter.html
The rack's SWARM · REF type is the reference's own code, unmodified. SWARM · PROPOSED is the same code plus the switches in its editor (P4 added by B287), each a spec change for the human, auditioned here and made nowhere else. The bank is rack-only (16 resonators and a physics tick): it is not a per-note candidate. Numbers are measured on fresh instances of the reference class (48 kHz, current bands/spread/placement, K = 0, drift 0).

P1Resonance is a volume knob. N unity-gain bandpasses summed capture less power as they narrow, so the whole curve sinks as qbase rises (reference :369-374). Shown: peak level swing across qbase 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.9. Q compensation — scale the bank sum by √(Qeff / Qmid), Qmid = the reference's default 2·√20, so the default is untouched and the knob stops moving the level.——
P2No low end. A sum of bandpasses has no DC path: anything below the lowest band is simply gone. Shown: how far 40 Hz sits below the curve's peak.LF keep — add back the input through a one-pole low-pass cornered at the lowest band.——
P3Modulation arrives as a slow glide. Moving center re-places the bands' HOME; the bands then follow on the home spring F = 1.5·(home − v) (reference :276), ≈ 0.67 s time constant, and every center write re-seeds the RNG (:210). Shown: time for the bank's mean to cover 90 % of a one-octave center step. Note for the ruling: src/filter_core.h:78-88 rebuild() snaps v = tHome on every structural write — the port jumps where this oracle glides (read from source, not measured here). Rigid modulation — keytrack and env TRANSPOSE the whole bank at coefficient time (v + Δ); the physics keeps running in unmodulated coordinates. ——
