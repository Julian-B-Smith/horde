fx-chain morph · round 2

B265 ROUND 2 LAB FOUR CORNERS · ONE MODULE PER TYPE · ADR-193 RULINGS D1–D8
Four FX chains from four different presets sit on the corners of an XY pad, with at most one module of each type (Drive, Filter, ECHO delay, Scape reverb, Shriek saturator, Bulwark dynamics, EQ, Sluice network). Drag the puck and the chain morphs through the round-2 brief's pipeline: presence votes, a lane-1 order target, a snap to a small approved rule set (lane 4's decode or lane 1's walk), corner sharpening γ, seeded jitter as temperature, and a router that changes one split group at a time under a +3 dB cap. Every new behaviour has a dev toggle for before/after listening; tails ring out to a per-module cap. It sounds, it measures itself live, and a self-check plants one fault per claim.
