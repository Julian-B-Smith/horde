Main: four layouts

Main has only ever been a stopgap: the voice, ENV 1 and a square envelope. These are four ways to make it the performance page, each with the bench's Specimen under an XY, the morphing logo in the header, and ENV 1's picture wide. Your picks become the ruling (B581).

Pictures' share: how much of the page's body the XY, the pad and the envelope take. The master asks for at least half on a page with pictures. Controls past their cell: how far Voice and ENV 1 spill out of the room the layout gives them, which is what a too-small cell does here (the page itself doesn't scroll). Morph on matters because the pad docks in a left column on every other page, unless the layout puts it on Main.
