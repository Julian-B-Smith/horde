Transposition ladders, the fine controls

A ladder is an ordered list of tiers. Each tier unlocks at its chaos threshold (From), reaches full weight over its Ramp, and is then drawn in proportion to its Weight. Intervals are in semitones from the row's default, as a list or a range like −48..48; an interval the rows can't reach is skipped. A row joins a ladder in the table's Ladder column; the ladder's first row in a source decides its roll.
