## Open questions for P4 / P5

- The thresholds above are provisional; the blind listening pass should set at least `alias`, `rough`, `rootAbsent` and `noisy` before P4 fits distributions to them.
- `overload6` is a JS-reference cost (B313: "a limit of the JS reference, not a verdict on the C++ port"). Should P4 bound N × blades by the JS cost, or wait for a C++ cost table?
- The aliasing reference is the engine's own 4× oversampling, whose blade caps move with it (gauntlet.mjs header): a cap-free reference needs the caps separated from `os` in the engine, which is out of this tool's reach.
- B346: the aliasing cut is to be re-fitted on `aliasConvDb` (the lead, 2026-09-29); THRESH still reads `aliasDb` here.
- `rough` counts a detuned swarm's own beating (Sethares roughness is fast beating, which is what a supersaw is). Should "discordance" for the random button exclude beating between members of one partial (e.g. measure roughness on a de-detuned render) or keep it?
- The harmonic detune law (`h.law` 4, `harmReach`) is the top `alias` and `noisy` hotspot: at E5 its upper members can sit near or above Nyquist. Is that a range to bound in P4, or a law to clamp in the engine?
- Edge-mode `silent` is mostly `gain` 0 and similar trivial extremes. Should P5's FUZZ mode exclude known-silent corners, or keep them as sanity cases?
