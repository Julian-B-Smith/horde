# Welcome and quick start

Purpose: orient a new player in one sitting. What horde is, what makes it different, how this
manual is organised, and a first sound within five minutes, with pointers to the chapters that go
deeper. A returning horde legacy user also learns here what changed and what did not carry over.

## What horde is

One page on the instrument: two SCALPEL oscillators on a coupled Kuramoto swarm, the Sub and a
noise oscillator, filters, a mixer, the FX rack, modulation, morph across four corners, and the
intent bus. The roster is frozen (ADR-190); the details wait on the build.
