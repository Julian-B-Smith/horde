MAP on: a drag still moves the value; click a knob to map the source at 25% (or focus its mapping); ⌥-drag sets the source's depth (release at 0 cancels). MAP off: ⌥-drag edits depth, ⌥-click cycles focus. The keys M, 1–9, P, Backspace and Esc act with KEYS ON (GUI 3 sets mapKeysAlways: always); Esc cancels one layer at a time (MAP, the pinned corner, keyboard mode). The inspector is their mouse route.
