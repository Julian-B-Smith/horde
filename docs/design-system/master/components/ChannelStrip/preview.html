Mix Each source is one strip: switch it on at the top, read its peak and its CLIP lamp beside each other, ride its fader with the meter right next to it, and mute or solo it just under the fader. Solo silences the other sources (dimmed, their M dashed); the master is never silenced. The fader is the house morph slider on its side, so its corner pips, tether and mod lanes show (OSC 2's level is mapped to LFO 1 here).

SAMPLE levels, generated in this preview from a seeded signal and the faders' own gains; never the engine. The lamp latches on a sample over 0 dBFS and clears on a click.
