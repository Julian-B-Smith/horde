Macro rows
Hardware wheels (sample)
Engine calls (onChange)

Drag the Bend row's slider and let go: it springs to the centre (a 50 ms time constant, in seconds) and its trace shows the return. Drag Mod wheel and let go: it stays. Hold the hardware range input: the row follows it and the mouse cannot move the row until you let go (the hardware wins); releasing the Bend input lets the row spring, releasing Mod leaves it. The scripted hand does the same on a loop. The log lists what the host's engine would be told (by mouse or spring); hardware values arrive from the engine and are never re-sent. Names of slots 5 to 8 are SAMPLES (ADR-192 leaves the phrases open).
