# Kuramoto circle

One voice's swarm on its phase ring, which is also the Coupling × Detune XY: where each member is in its cycle, where its blades cut, how locked the swarm is, and the point you set it with. Drawn from the SCALPEL interface lab's ring, not gui2's phase circle (the human, 2026-10-22).

**Looks** The canvas is wider than tall (4:3), so the rim arcs and the R bar have room. The ring in `scr-grid` 30%; phase p sits at angle 2πp from 3 o'clock, counter-clockwise (y up). Members are dots of radius 4.5 in `scr-physics`, **filled `scr-value` while the member is inside one of its own blade windows**. Each member's blade windows are **rim arcs** outside the ring, one concentric arc per member (member 1 innermost, 2.2 px apart), 1.8 px in `scr-value`: blade 1 solid at 85%, blade 2 dashed at 55%. The **mean-field needle** runs from the centre to R times the ring's radius, 2 px. **One ink for the needle, the Coherence readout and the R bar** (B323): `scr-meter` at full weight, in every scheme and theme, because the number is what names it. The readout ("Coherence 0.71", with "7 members" under it in `scr-grid`) sits top right; the R bar stands at the right edge under it, 5 px, a track in `scr-grid`, filled from the bottom to R, with a hairline at ½ outside the track. The **cross-mod arrows**, when cross-mod is on: member i+1 pushes member i, each arrow bowing toward the centre so two coincident members (a locked swarm) still read as a small loop. An arrow's **weight is the effect** of that push on member i's output, not the knob: width 0.9 to 3.5 px, alpha 30% to 95%, head growing with it, in `scr-meter`; a push that lands outside every window has no effect and is a dotted hairline at 35%. The **XY layer** is drawn first, under the ring: "lock" at top left and "splay" at bottom left (Coupling's two poles), "detune →" at bottom right, all `scr-grid` at 90%; two short edge ticks where K = 0 (mid-height), not a line through the ring; a faint `scr-value` crosshair (20%) with a tick on the left and bottom rails; and the point itself, a **hollow puck** (radius 6.5, 1.8 px, 2.4 px while dragging, a 1.4 px centre dot), hollow so a member under it still shows. Each frame clears the ground; there are no trails.

**Gestures** (B294, B295; `Horde.RingXY`) The ring's canvas **is** the parameter plane: Detune runs across the whole canvas, Coupling up it, so the point is wherever the pointer is.
- **A click lands.** ↔ Detune and ↕ Coupling go to the clicked point (absolute), and the drag follows the pointer. Each is `hand + offset`, clamped, so coming back from the edge never drifts from the hand.
- **alt is fine:** the value moves ¼ as far as the hand, and letting go of alt keeps the offset, so the drag goes on 1:1 from where it was.
- **⇧ drags the third axis, Cross-mod, relative:** one canvas width is the whole range (¼ with alt), ↔ holds while it does, and a ⇧-grab lands nothing.
- Arrows move the point (alt fine, ⇧ + ← → Cross-mod). The canvas is one slider whose value text names Detune, Coupling, Cross-mod and Coherence; its keyboard focus ring is drawn on the well, outside the ink edge.
- A readout line beside the ring (never over it): idle it gives the values; on hover "click lands ↔ Detune ↕ Coupling here · alt = fine (¼) · ⇧↔ Cross-mod is relative"; while dragging "DRAG · ↔ Detune 70 % · ↕ Coupling +40 %". The Coherence number is repeated in `value` text beside it, because ORCHID's `scr-meter` is a mark colour (under 4.5:1) and the canvas number is a mark. One writer for the gesture; one undo node per drag.

**Fed by** RC-8: per voice and oscillator, the members' phases and each blade's window (position and width), the snapshot about 60 Hz; the view computes Coherence from the phases itself, so the needle, the readout and the bar can only agree. The point is the oscillator's Detune and Coupling manifest keys (Coupling −1..1, 0 at mid-height; Detune 0..1) and the third axis its Cross-mod. **OPEN:** an arrow's weight needs each member's cross-mod effect (what the push adds to its output), and RC-8 does not publish one today. Until it does, the view draws a placeholder; the preview scales a seeded strength by how much of the cycle the member's windows cover. The preview's swarm is a small seeded Kuramoto (7 members, mulberry32), never the engine: Detune spreads its natural frequencies, Coupling is K in either sign, and a click visibly locks or splays it.

**Voice-bound or audio-capable** Voice-bound: it needs a sounding voice, and the Detune and Coupling it edits belong to an oscillator. The FX device (ADR-254) has no voice, so it does not show this view.

**Screens** drawn only with the six screen roles, so it follows the Screen scheme; glow is a wider underlay stroke or disc, never a blur (this view needs none); scanlines at the scheme's strength.

**Reduced motion** one still frame, redrawn only when its data or the scheme changes, which includes a click or an arrow: the preview settles a fresh swarm for three seconds at the chosen point and draws once.

**Fits** its canvas fills the column's width at a fixed aspect (4:3) and draws at the display's pixel ratio (capped at 2), so it never runs past its card.

**Sources** the SCALPEL interface lab's phase ring (`docs/design/scalpel-interface-lab.html`: `paintRing`, `ringXYLayer`, `ringRBar`, `ringWire`, `absGrab` and `absStep`; B294 the ring is the Coupling × Detune XY, B295 a click lands absolutely, B323 one ink for needle, readout and bar); UI Spec §6 KURAMOTO; Vaporwave Phosphor. Not carried over from the lab: its XY mapping row (remapping the axes), a blade role as the target (the ring targets blades by the arc under the pointer), the macro-reach box and the carried-value diamond. They belong to the mapping and modulation views, and this ring's axes are fixed at Detune, Coupling and Cross-mod.

The preview draws SAMPLE data generated in the preview (seeded, deterministic), never the engine.
