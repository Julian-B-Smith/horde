Chain lineAUDIO-CAPABLE

x is the chain in signal order, y each tap’s peak hold (−48 to +6 dBFS; below −48 a tap sits on the floor line); the dashed rail is 0 dBFS. The dots are readings and never move a gain. Point at one (or focus the line and use ← →) to name it. The four source lanes share the first column, side by side, and fold into the next stage, then run through the rack. The bars under the line are the controls: one per stage with a gain, in its dot’s column, rising from the 0 dB line for a boost and falling for a cut, all on one dB scale (equal dB, equal height). Drag a bar up or down (shift for fine, double-click for the default, ↑ ↓ on a focused bar); it moves only when you drag it or move its Gain staging fader below, never with the levels. A stage with no gain shows an empty slot. On a narrow screen the line and its lane scroll together, so a dot and its bar stay in one column. Sources + output fed is a preview state: the taps nothing publishes yet have no reading, so they are hollow on the floor, never a guess (and silent in Gain staging).
