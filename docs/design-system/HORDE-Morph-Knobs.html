HORDE Morph Knobs

Morph knob prototype. Move the puck to blend between corners. A knob's neutral arc is the blended value. The colored pips on the track are each corner's own value, sized by that corner's weight. Each parameter has its own stochastic boundary, so knobs change owner at different moments (a halo marks each change; turn Drift on to watch). Dragging a knob edits the editing corner (the outlined pip, tethered to the blended value). The blend then moves by that corner's weight. ⌥-drag edits that corner's mod depth (Cutoff and Fold have one).
