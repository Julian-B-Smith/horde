HORDE Slider Marks

Slider version of map mode and morph knobs. Mod rings become lanes stacked under (or beside) the track, with the same rules: each lane starts at the live output of the lanes inside it, has an origin dot (at the end for unipolar and inverse, in the middle for bipolar) and a live tick. Morph pips sit on the track. The thumb is the editing corner: it is drawn in that corner's color, at that corner's stored value, and dashed-tethered to the blended value (the end of the neutral fill).
